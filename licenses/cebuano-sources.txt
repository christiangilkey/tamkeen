Bisaya (Cebuano) word list and dictionary (optional downloads)

1. Bisaya meanings for "No AI", second file (docs/lexicon/ceb-wolff-meanings.json.gz)

   From John U. Wolff, A Dictionary of Cebuano Visayan (Cornell University Southeast Asia Program
   and the Linguistic Society of the Philippines, 1972), as transcribed for Project Gutenberg
   (ebook 40074, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/40074) by Jeroen Hellingman, Scott Robertson
   and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team. Project Gutenberg marks it "Public domain in the
   USA". Tamkeen keeps each entry's meanings (not its example sentences) and shows the words in
   today's spelling; the words an entry lists only by their affix (under balay, "paniN- household")
   are spelled out by the rules of Wolff's introduction (panimalay) and kept when that spelling is in
   the word list below; tools/build_cebuano_glosses.py says exactly how.

2. Bisaya word list for finding roots, and the 1000 most common Bisaya words
   (docs/lexicon/ceb-roots.json.gz, web/js/wordlists/ceb.js)

   Counted from the Cebuano monolingual text of the NLLB collection as distributed by OPUS
   (https://opus.nlpl.eu/NLLB, https://object.pouta.csc.fi/OPUS-NLLB/v1/mono/ceb.txt.gz), released
   under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By 1.0,
   https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). Only word counts are kept, never the text.

   J. Tiedemann, 2012, Parallel Data, Tools and Interfaces in OPUS. In Proceedings of LREC 2012.
   NLLB Team et al., 2022, No Language Left Behind: Scaling Human-Centered Machine Translation.
