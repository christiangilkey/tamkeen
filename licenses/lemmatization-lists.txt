Spanish, French, Italian and English word tables (optional download)

People learning Spanish, French, Italian or English can download a table of word forms and their
dictionary forms (hablas -> hablar), so every form of a word counts as the same word. The tables
are not part of this program; the app downloads them only when the learner agrees.

They are made from:

  Lemmatization Lists by Michal Měchura, https://github.com/michmech/lemmatization-lists
  Available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  (The lists themselves were collected from free sources: Hunspell dictionaries, Lexique,
  FreeLing, Multext East and others; see the project's README.)

  wordfreq by Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq (CC BY-SA 4.0): its word counts
  decide which forms are kept and which dictionary form a spelling with two is given.

The tables are derived databases and are distributed under the Open Database License 1.0. The
files, the full licence text and how to rebuild them:
https://github.com/christiangilkey/tamkeen/tree/main/docs/lexicon
