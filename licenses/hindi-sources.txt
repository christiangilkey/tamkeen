Hindi word table, meanings and readings (optional downloads)

1. Hindi word table (docs/lexicon/hi-lemmas.json.gz, made by tools/build_hindi.py)

   Every Hindi word of its own in the English Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), the forms in
   its inflection table (nouns: plural and oblique; adjectives: feminine and plural; verbs: their
   participles, future, subjunctive, imperative and infinitive forms), the entries that are forms or
   other spellings of another word, and Wiktionary's romanization of the spellings whose reading the
   app's own rule (web/js/lexicon/hindi.js) gets wrong. Written by the Wiktionary contributors and
   extracted by Wiktextract (Tatu Ylonen, https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract; data from
   https://kaikki.org). Wiktionary's text is available under the Creative Commons
   Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
   and the GNU Free Documentation License; the table is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

   The words are put in order, and a spelling that is a form of two words is given to the likelier
   one, by the word counts of wordfreq (Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), whose data
   is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 too.

2. Hindi meanings for "No AI" (docs/lexicon/hi-meanings.json.gz, made by tools/build_meanings.py)

   The same English Wiktionary data (see wiktionary.txt): each word's meanings, part of speech and
   reading, the forms of its inflection table, and Hindi's postpositions of more than one word
   (के लिए "for", के बाद "after") under their main word.
