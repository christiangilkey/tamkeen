Russian word table with stress marks (optional download)

People learning Russian can download a table of word forms, their dictionary forms and where
their stress falls, so every form of a word (and both verbs of an aspect pair) counts as the same
word, and Russian text can show its stress (ла́дно). The table is not part of this program; the
app downloads it only when the learner agrees.

It is made from the OpenRussian.org dictionary data, https://github.com/Badestrand/russian-dictionary
(compiled by the OpenRussian.org team and users, much of it from Wiktionary), licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Word counts from wordfreq by Robyn Speer (CC BY-SA 4.0) decide which forms are kept.

What was changed: only the word forms, their dictionary forms, stress positions and aspect pairs
were kept (no translations or sentences), and perfective verbs are listed under their
imperfective partner. The table is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The file, the licence and how
to rebuild it: https://github.com/christiangilkey/tamkeen/tree/main/docs/lexicon
