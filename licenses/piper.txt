   Piper's download contains, as parts of that separate program: eSpeak NG (GPL-3.0,
   https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng), which turns text into sounds; ONNX Runtime (MIT,
   Microsoft); piper-phonemize (MIT) and libtashkeel (MIT), which adds vowel marks to Arabic text
   before it is spoken. Their licences come inside that download. Tamkeen only starts the program;
   none of it is part of Tamkeen.

2. One voice per language, from https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices (each voice's
   MODEL_CARD names the licence of its recordings):

     it_IT-serena-medium     Italian     CC BY 4.0

     en_US-ljspeech-medium   English     public domain (LJ Speech)
     en_GB-cori-medium       English     public domain

   Many "medium" Piper voices were trained on top of Piper's English "lessac" voice, whose own
   recordings are licensed for research; Piper publishes each voice under its own recordings'
   licence. Voices whose recordings are licensed for non-commercial use only are not offered.
