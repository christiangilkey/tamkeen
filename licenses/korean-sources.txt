Korean word list, its counts, and the 1000 most common Korean words (optional download)

1. The words (docs/lexicon/ko-words.json.gz)

   The Korean entries of the English Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), as extracted by
   Wiktextract (https://kaikki.org, Tatu Ylonen, "Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured
   Data", LREC 2022): each one-word Korean entry, its part of speech, and for verbs and adjectives the
   two stems their endings go on, taken from the entry's conjugation table. Wiktionary's text is licensed
   under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
   (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the GNU Free Documentation License.

2. How common each word is (the order of the list above, and web/js/wordlists/ko.js)

   Counted from the Korean film and TV subtitles of OpenSubtitles 2018 (http://www.opensubtitles.org/,
   through OPUS: P. Lison and J. Tiedemann, 2016, "OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large Parallel Corpora
   from Movie and TV Subtitles", LREC 2016), as counted by Hermit Dave's FrequencyWords
   (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, ko_full.txt), whose word counts are licensed CC BY-SA
   4.0. Tamkeen reads each counted word with its own rules (web/js/lexicon/korean.js) and adds its count
   to the dictionary word it is a form of (먹었어요 counts for 먹다); tools/build_korean_words.mjs says
   exactly how. A few common nouns Wiktionary lacks are added from the counts. Only counts are kept,
   never any subtitle text. The 1000 most common words keep only words Wiktionary has.

The word list and the common-word list are shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0, with credit
as above. This covers these lists only, not the rest of Tamkeen.

3. How Korean words are said (shown over Korean text and in the word popup)

   Worked out by the app from the letters, following the Revised Romanization of Korean (Ministry of
   Culture and Tourism, Republic of Korea, 2000), an official public standard. No data is downloaded
   for it.

4. Korean meanings for "No AI" (docs/lexicon/ko-meanings.json.gz): see wiktionary.txt.
