Chinese dictionary: CC-CEDICT with word counts from wordfreq (optional download)

People learning Chinese can download a dictionary that splits Chinese text into words, gives each
word's pinyin, and treats simplified and traditional characters as the same word. It is not part
of this program; the app downloads it only when the learner agrees (docs/lexicon/zh-cedict.json.gz).

It is made from CC-CEDICT (https://cc-cedict.org, published by MDBG, https://www.mdbg.net),
"Community maintained free Chinese-English dictionary", the edition of 2026-10-17:

    CEDICT - Copyright (C) 1997, 1998 Paul Andrew Denisowski
    CC-CEDICT: the CC-CEDICT contributors

and from the Chinese word counts of wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer
(https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq; Robyn Speer. (2022). rspeer/wordfreq: v3.0. Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7199437), which decide which way of cutting a sentence into words
is likeliest.

and so is the dictionary file made from them:

    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    (full text: docs/lexicon/LICENSE-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt)

What was changed: only the words, their pinyin and their simplified and traditional spellings were
kept (the English definitions were dropped); one reading was chosen for each word (common readings
of a few characters that have several, like 了 le, were set by hand); a word count was added to
each word; words with letters or digits were left out; and the result was written as gzipped JSON
by tools/build_cjk.py.
