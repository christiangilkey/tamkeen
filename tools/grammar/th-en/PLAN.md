# Thai → English grammar: the plan

American English explained in Thai, for a Thai speaker who knows no grammar. The explanations are
written in natural Thai, the way a good Thai teacher explains English to Thai students (the usual Thai
grammar words: คำนาม, คำกริยา, ประธาน, รูปอดีต…, each explained the first time), and every comparison is
with Thai. Examples are everyday American English (restroom, apartment, Mom, color); their translations
are natural Thai. No readings (`"reading": ""`).

## What is hard for a Thai speaker, and so gets the most time

- **Verbs change in English; Thai verbs never do.** am/is/are, the -s of *he works*, -ing, the past
  (*-ed* and the irregular ones), *have been*. Thai shows time with words (เมื่อวาน, แล้ว, กำลัง, จะ):
  English needs both the word and the verb form (*Yesterday I went*, not *Yesterday I go*).
- **"be" with adjectives**: Thai says เขาสูง; English must say *He **is** tall*. And the opposite
  mistake: *I am go*, *I am agree*.
- **do in questions and negatives**: Thai adds ไหม at the end and ไม่ before the verb; English needs
  *Do you…?*, *I don't…*, *Did you…?*.
- **a / an / the and plurals**: Thai has neither; classifiers do some of the work. *two book* → *two
  books*, *I like dog* → *I like dogs*.
- **he / she**: เขา is both; *his / her* too.
- **The subject is always said**: ร้อนจัง → *It's so hot*; ไปแล้ว → *I'm leaving*.
- **Word order inside the noun**: Thai puts the describing word after (รถสีแดง, แม่ของฉัน); English
  before (*a red car*, *my mother*). Question words go first in English (*Where are you going?*, not
  *You go where?*).
- **Verbs in a row**: Thai ไปซื้อ, อยากไป, ชอบว่ายน้ำ → English *go (to) buy*, *want **to** go*, *like
  swimm**ing***.
- **Answers to negative questions**: ไม่ไปเหรอ — ใช่ (ไม่ไป) → *Aren't you going? — **No**, I'm not.*
- **เคย, ได้, ให้**: one Thai word, several English ones (*ever / used to*; *can / didn't*; *give / let /
  make / for*).
- **Pronunciation notes**: final consonants (Thai ends words only in p, t, k, m, n, ng and the vowels):
  the *-s* of plurals and *he works*, the *-ed* of the past, *l* at the end (*school*, not สกูน), *v* and
  *w*, *th*, *r* and *l*, clusters (*street*, not สะตรีท).

## Units and lessons

Levels: lessons 1–20 A1, 21–41 A2, 42–52 B1.

### Unit 1. บทสนทนาแรก (First conversations), A1
| # | id | capability | grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | hello-im | Say hello and who you are | *I'm*, *my name is*; politeness without ครับ / ค่ะ; *I* with a capital |
| 2 | where-youre-from | Say where you're from and what you do | *I am / you are*; *a* + a job (*I'm a nurse*); *from* |
| 3 | he-she-they | Talk about other people | *he* / *she* (เขา is both), *they*, *is / are*; *his / her* |
| 4 | its-good | Say what things are like | *be* + adjective (*The food is good*); *it* as a subject (*It's hot*); *very, really, so* |
| 5 | are-you | Ask yes/no questions with *be*, and answer | *Are you…? Is it…?*; *Yes, I am. / No, it isn't.*; *not* |
| 6 | whats-this | Point and name: this, that, a, an | *What's this? It's a…*; *this / that*; *a / an* |
| 7 | more-than-one | Talk about more than one | plural *-s / -es* (and saying the s); *people, children*; *these / those*; no classifiers |

### Unit 2. ออกไปข้างนอก (Out and about), A1
| # | id | capability | grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | can-i-have | Ask for things politely | *Can I have…, please?*, *I'd like…*; *a / some*; *please* and *thank you* in place of ครับ / ค่ะ |
| 9 | want-to | Say what you want and want to do | *want* + thing; *want to* + verb (not *want go*); *would like to* |
| 10 | there-is | Say what there is: there is / there are | มี = *there is* (not *have*); *Is there…?*; *There aren't any* |
| 11 | where-is-it | Say where things are | *be* + *in, on, under, next to, between, across from, near* |
| 12 | how-much-many | Shop: How much? How many? | *How much is / are…?*; *How many* + plural; *a cup of, a bottle of* (instead of classifiers) |
| 13 | give-directions | Give and follow directions | the verb alone (*Go straight*, *Turn left*), *Don't…*; *Excuse me* |

### Unit 3. ชีวิตประจำวัน (Everyday life), A1
| # | id | capability | grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | daily-routine | Say what you do every day | present simple: the verb with *I / you / we / they*; *at* + time; *every day* |
| 15 | he-works | Say what other people do | *-s* with *he / she / it* (*works, watches, goes*); saying the s |
| 16 | dont-doesnt | Say what you don't do | *don't / doesn't* + verb (not *I not…*, not *He don't*) |
| 17 | do-you | Ask Do you…? and answer Yes, I do | *Do / Does…?* (instead of ไหม); short answers |
| 18 | have-has | Say what you have, and whose it is | *have / has*; *Do you have…?*; *my, your, his, her, our, their*; *'s* (*Nok's*); the owner first (*my mom*, not *mom my*) |
| 19 | wh-questions | Ask where, when, what and who | the question word first: *Where do you live?* (not *You live where?*) |
| 20 | like-ing | Say what you like doing, and how often | *like / love / hate* + *-ing*; *always, usually, sometimes, never* |

### Unit 4. ตอนนี้และวันข้างหน้า (Now and what's next), A2
| # | id | capability | grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | right-now | Say what you're doing right now | *am / is / are* + *-ing* (กำลัง...อยู่); spelling *-ing* |
| 22 | usually-or-now | Say what you usually do and what you're doing now | present simple vs continuous; verbs without *-ing* (*want, like, know*) |
| 23 | can-cant | Say what you can and can't do | *can / can't* + verb (ได้, เป็น, สามารถ: all *can*); *Can you…?*; answering *Yes, I can* (not *Can*) |
| 24 | going-to | Talk about your plans | *be going to* + verb; *this weekend, next week* |
| 25 | will | Offer, promise and guess: will | *will / won't*; *I'll…*; *will* or *going to* |
| 26 | have-to-should | Say what you have to and should do | *have to / don't have to*, *must / mustn't*, *should* (ต้อง, ไม่ต้อง, ห้าม, ควร) |
| 27 | invite-and-ask | Invite, suggest and ask favors | *Would you like…?*, *Let's…*, *How about…?*, *Could you…?* |

### Unit 5. เล่าเรื่องที่ผ่านมา (Telling what happened), A2
| # | id | capability | grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28 | was-were | Say where you were and how it was | *was / were*; *Where were you?* |
| 29 | past-ed | Say what you did: the regular past | *-ed* (and its three sounds); time words are not enough |
| 30 | past-irregular | Say what you did: the common irregular verbs | *went, ate, saw, bought, had, got, took, made…* |
| 31 | did-didnt | Ask and say no about the past | *Did you…?*, *I didn't* + verb; ไม่ได้ไป = *didn't go* (not *can't go*) |
| 32 | tell-a-story | Tell a story | *first, then, after that*; the past continuous for the background (*I was walking home when…*) |
| 33 | used-to | Say how things used to be | *used to* (เคย for a habit), *didn't use to* |

### Unit 6. เวลา ปริมาณ และการเปรียบเทียบ (Time, amounts and comparing), A2
| # | id | capability | grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 34 | at-on-in | Say when and where: at, on, in | *at 7, on Monday, in July*; *at home, in Bangkok, on the bus* |
| 35 | a-or-the | Say a, the, or nothing | *a* (one of many), *the* (the one we both know), nothing (*I like dogs*, *go to school*) |
| 36 | some-any-much-many | Say how much and how many | countable and uncountable nouns; *some / any*; *much / many / a lot of* |
| 37 | compare | Compare two things | *-er than*, *more … than*, *as … as*; *better, worse*; not *more bigger* |
| 38 | the-best | Say which is the best | *the -est*, *the most*; *the best, the worst*; *in / of* |
| 39 | too-enough | Describe things: before the noun, too, enough | adjective before the noun (*a red car*); *too* (เกินไป), *enough* (พอ) |

### Unit 7. ประสบการณ์และการเชื่อมความคิด (Experiences and connecting ideas), A2–B1
| # | id | capability | grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 | have-you-ever | Talk about experiences | present perfect: *Have you ever been…?*, *I've never…* (not *I ever went*) |
| 41 | for-since | Say how long: for and since | *I've lived here for three years / since 2020* (not *I live here three years already*) |
| 42 | just-already-yet | Say what's just happened, and what hasn't yet | *just, already, yet* (เพิ่ง, แล้ว, ยัง); *Have you eaten yet?* |
| 43 | because-so-although | Connect ideas: because, so, but, although | *because, so, but, although* (not *Although…, but…*) |
| 44 | if-will | Say what will happen if… | *If + present, will* (not *If it will rain*) |
| 45 | negative-questions | Answer negative questions and check things | *Don't you…? — No, I don't.*; *…, right?*, *isn't it?* |

### Unit 8. ความคิดเห็น จินตนาการ และภาษาอังกฤษแบบเจ้าของภาษา (Opinions, imagination and natural English), B1
| # | id | capability | grammar |
|---|---|---|---|
| 46 | who-which-that | Describe people and things with a whole sentence | *who, which, that* (ที่) |
| 47 | said-that | Pass on what someone said | *said (that)…*, *told me to…*, *asked if…*; the tense moves back |
| 48 | opinions | Give your opinion, agree and disagree | *I think…*, *In my opinion…*, *I agree* (not *I am agree*), *I'm not sure* |
| 49 | if-i-were | Imagine: If I had…, I would… | the second conditional; *I wish…* |
| 50 | phrasal-verbs | Use everyday phrasal verbs | *turn on / off* (not *open the light*), *pick up, look for, find out, give up, get up* |
| 51 | let-make-have | Say ให้ in English: give, let, make, have someone do | *let* + person + verb, *make* + person + verb, *ask / tell* + person + *to*, *for* |
| 52 | passive | Understand the passive: it was built, it's made | *be* + past participle; neutral, not only unlucky (unlike ถูก / โดน) |

## The spiral: where each big structure comes back

- **be**: 1–5 (am / is / are), 11 (where things are), 21 (*be* + -ing), 24 (*be going to*), 28 (*was / were*), 32 (*was walking*), 52 (*be* + past participle).
- **do**: 16, 17, 19 (questions), 18 (*Do you have…?*), 22, 31 (*did*), 45 (negative questions).
- **-s**: 7 (plurals), 15 (*he works*), 18 (*has*), 36 (countable nouns), 22.
- **Questions**: 5 (*be*), 10 (*Is there…?*), 12 (*How much…?*), 17, 19, 23 (*Can you…?*), 31 (*Did you…?*), 40 (*Have you ever…?*), 42 (*yet?*), 45, 47 (*asked if…*).
- **The past**: 28–33, 40–42 (the present perfect, and when the past simple is used instead), 47 (reported speech), 49 (the past for imagining), 52.
- **Articles**: 2 (*a* + job), 6 (*a / an*), 8 (*a / some*), 12, 35 (*a, the* or nothing), 38 (*the best*).
- **Verbs after verbs**: 9 (*want to*), 20 (*like -ing*), 27 (*would like to*, *How about -ing*), 33 (*used to*), 51 (*let me go*, *ask me to go*).
- **Modal verbs**: 23 (*can*), 25 (*will*), 26 (*should, must*), 27 (*could, would*), 44 (*will* in conditionals), 49 (*would*).
- **เคย, ได้, ให้**: 23 and 31 (ได้: *can* or *didn't*), 33 and 40 (เคย: *used to* or *ever*), 51 (ให้).
- **Word order**: 4 and 39 (the adjective before the noun), 18 (*my mom*), 19 (the question word first), 46 (who / which / that after the noun, as Thai ที่).
