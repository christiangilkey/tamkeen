# English → Tagalog: the plan

Tagalog (Filipino) as spoken in Manila, explained in English, from zero to everyday conversation (A2,
touching B1). Spelling as the Tagalog basics course writes it (`kumusta`, `dito`, `pwede`, `iyan`,
`piso`, `kuwento`); everyday Taglish where Manila speakers really use it (a lesson of its own in unit 5).

## How Tagalog is taught here, and why in this order

- **No "to be", and the important word comes first.** *Guro siya* "teacher she" = "She's a teacher".
  From lesson 1, sentences are built predicate-first; the *ay* order (*Siya ay guro*) is shown only to
  recognise (writing, speeches).
- **The markers are learned through what they do**, a little at a time, never as a table to memorise
  on day one: *si* before a name (1), *ang* "the one we're talking about" (3, 5), *ng* "some / of"
  (7, 10), *sa* "at / to / in" (11), then their real job in verbs: who or what the sentence is about
  (12 onward, and the whole of unit 5). *si/ni/kay* and *sina* run alongside.
- **Three pronoun sets** arrive as needed: *ako, ikaw/ka, siya* (1, 3), *kami / tayo / kayo / sila* (6),
  *ko, mo, niya…* after *gusto* (7) and as "my, your" (10), *akin, iyo…* (10, 11), the *ko/mo* doer
  with object-focus verbs (24), and *kita* (26).
- **The linker *na / -ng*** joins words: *gusto kong* (7), *dalawang anak, mayroon akong* (8),
  *magandang bahay / bahay na maganda* (9), *anong oras* (16), *huwag kang* (17), *kailangan kong* (23),
  *ang librong binili ko* (39).
- **Verbs by aspect, not tense**: has the action started, and is it finished? Ongoing (12), completed
  (13), not yet begun (14), habit (15); recent past *kakakain* (30), experience *nakapunta na* (31),
  past habit (46), a whole story (47).
- **Actor focus first** (*-um-, mag-, ma-*: units 3–4), **object focus after** (*-in, i-, -an*: unit 5),
  then ability and accident *ma-/maka-* (32, 33, 45), causative *pa-* (44).
- **Politeness from day one**: *po / opo* (2), then *po* in every scene with an older person or a
  stranger; polite requests *paki-, pwede po ba, pabili po* (20).
- **The little words** (*ba, na, pa, din/rin, lang, naman, nga, pala, daw/raw, yata, muna*) appear early
  in chunks; their meanings and their order get a lesson of their own (38) once the learner has many.

## Units and lessons

Levels: lessons 1–19 A1, 20–38 A2, 39–48 B1 (19 / 19 / 10).

### Unit 1: First conversations (A1)
| # | id | capability | grammar | level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `say-who-you-are` | Say hello and who you are | no "to be"; *ako, ikaw/ka*; *si* before a name; *taga-* | A1 |
| 2 | `speak-politely` | Speak politely to older people and strangers | *po / opo*; *kayo* as polite "you"; where *po* goes | A1 |
| 3 | `say-what-someone-is` | Say what someone is and what they're like | predicate first; *siya* "he/she"; *ang* before a noun; *ay* to recognise | A1 |
| 4 | `ask-yes-no-questions` | Ask yes-or-no questions and answer them | *ba*; *oo / hindi*; *hindi* + pronoun order | A1 |
| 5 | `point-and-name-things` | Point at things and name them | *ito, iyan, iyon*; *ano*; *ang* = the; *mga* | A1 |

### Unit 2: People, things and places (A1)
| 6 | `say-we-and-they` | Talk about yourselves and other people | *kami* vs *tayo*; *kayo, sila*; *sina* | A1 |
| 7 | `say-what-you-want` | Say what you want | *gusto / ayaw* + *ko, mo, niya*; *ng* "some"; *gusto kong* + verb (linker *-ng*) | A1 |
| 8 | `say-what-you-have` | Say what you have and what there is | *may, mayroon, wala*; numbers 1–10 with the linker | A1 |
| 9 | `describe-people-and-things` | Describe people and things | the linker *na/-ng* with adjectives; *ma-* adjectives; *Ang ganda!*; *napaka-* | A1 |
| 10 | `say-whose-it-is` | Say whose something is | *bahay ko / aking bahay / akin ito*; *ng, ni*; *kanino* | A1 |
| 11 | `say-where-things-are` | Say where people and things are | *nasa, nasaan*; *nandito / nandiyan / nandoon*; *sa loob ng, sa tabi ng…* | A1 |

### Unit 3: Doing things (A1)
| 12 | `say-what-youre-doing` | Say what you're doing right now | verbs; aspect; ongoing *-um-* and *mag-* (*kumakain, nagluluto*) | A1 |
| 13 | `say-what-you-did` | Say what you did | completed aspect (*kumain, nagluto*); *na* and *pa*; *Kumain ka na ba?* | A1 |
| 14 | `say-what-you-will-do` | Say what you're going to do | not-yet-begun aspect (*kakain, magluluto*); the full table | A1 |
| 15 | `talk-about-your-day` | Talk about your daily routine | habit with the ongoing form; *ma-* verbs (*matulog, maligo*); time first | A1 |
| 16 | `ask-questions` | Ask who, what, where, when, how | question words; the little pronoun moves up (*Saan ka nakatira?*) | A1 |
| 17 | `tell-people-what-to-do` | Tell someone what to do (and not to) | commands; *huwag kang*; *tayo* "let's"; *Tara!* | A1 |

### Unit 4: Out and about (A1 → A2)
| 18 | `count-and-pay` | Count, and ask how much | native numbers + linker; *ilan*; *magkano*; Spanish numbers for prices | A1 |
| 19 | `tell-the-time` | Ask and tell the time and the day | *Anong oras na?*; *alas-*; days; *sa Lunes* | A1 |
| 20 | `ask-for-things-politely` | Ask for things politely | *paki-*; *pwede po ba*; *pabili po*; *makikiraan po* | A2 |
| 21 | `find-your-way` | Ask the way and get around by jeep | *paano pumunta sa*; *kumanan, kumaliwa*; *sumakay, bumaba*; *Para po!* | A2 |
| 22 | `make-plans` | Invite someone and make plans | *Gusto mo bang…?*; *mag-* together (*magkita tayo*); accepting and refusing | A2 |
| 23 | `say-what-you-need-to-do` | Say what you need to, should, may and mustn't do | *kailangan, dapat, pwede, bawal* + verb | A2 |

### Unit 5: Putting the thing in focus (A2)
| 24 | `focus-on-the-thing` | Talk about a particular thing you ate, bought, saw | object focus *-in*; *Kumain ako ng mangga* vs *Kinain ko ang mangga* | A2 |
| 25 | `put-and-give-things` | Say where to put things and who to give them to | *i-* focus (*ilagay, ibigay, isara*) | A2 |
| 26 | `do-things-to-people-and-places` | Call, help, visit and open | *-an* focus (*tawagan, tulungan, puntahan, buksan*); *kita* | A2 |
| 27 | `ask-who-did-what` | Ask who did it and what they did | *Sino ang kumain?* vs *Ano ang kinain mo?*: the questioned word is *ang* | A2 |
| 28 | `use-english-words-the-tagalog-way` | Use English words the Tagalog way (Taglish) | *mag-text, i-send, na-late* | A2 |

### Unit 6: Telling what happened (A2)
| 29 | `tell-what-happened` | Tell someone what you did yesterday | completed forms of every focus in a row; *tapos, pagkatapos*; time words | A2 |
| 30 | `say-you-just-did-it` | Say you've just done something | *kakakain ko lang, kararating niya* | A2 |
| 31 | `talk-about-experiences` | Say what you've done (and never done) | *nakapunta na / hindi pa nakakapunta* | A2 |
| 32 | `say-what-you-saw-and-heard` | Say what you saw, heard, understood and know | *makita, marinig, maintindihan*; *alam / kilala* | A2 |
| 33 | `say-what-you-can-do` | Say what you can and can't do | *marunong, kaya, maka-* | A2 |
| 34 | `say-how-you-feel` | Say how you feel | *gutom / nagugutom*, *inaantok*; *nakakatuwa* "it makes one…" | A2 |

### Unit 7: Connecting your ideas (A2 → B1)
| 35 | `say-why` | Say why | *kasi, dahil, kaya*; *Bakit naman?* | A2 |
| 36 | `say-when` | Say when and in what order | *kapag/pag, noong, habang, bago, pagkatapos* | A2 |
| 37 | `compare-things` | Compare people and things | *mas … kaysa, pinaka-, kasing-* | A2 |
| 38 | `sound-natural-with-little-words` | Sound natural with the little words | *lang, naman, nga, pala, muna, yata, din/rin*; their order | A2 |
| 39 | `describe-with-clauses` | Say which one: "the man who…, the book I bought" | relative clauses with the linker | B1 |

### Unit 8: Stories, opinions and imagination (B1)
| 40 | `report-what-people-say` | Pass on what someone said | *daw/raw*; *sabi ni*; *kung* "whether" | B1 |
| 41 | `give-your-opinion` | Say what you think | *sa tingin ko, para sa akin, akala ko, mukhang*; agreeing | B1 |
| 42 | `talk-about-if` | Talk about what will happen if… | *kung, kapag* + not-yet-begun; *kung ako sa iyo* | B1 |
| 43 | `make-wishes` | Wish, hope and regret | *sana*; *sayang* | B1 |
| 44 | `get-things-done` | Have something done, make someone do it | causative *magpa-, pa-…-in* | B1 |
| 45 | `say-it-was-an-accident` | Say something happened by accident | *ma-* (*nabasag, naiwan, nahulog*) | B1 |
| 46 | `talk-about-how-things-were` | Say how things used to be | *dati, noon, noong bata pa ako* + ongoing form | B1 |
| 47 | `tell-a-story` | Tell a story | *isang araw, habang, bigla, sa wakas*; aspects together | B1 |
| 48 | `keep-a-conversation-going` | Keep a conversation going | follow-up questions; *Talaga? E ikaw? Tapos?* | B1 |
