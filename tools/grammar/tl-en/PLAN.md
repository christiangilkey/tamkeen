# Tagalog → English: the plan

American English for a Tagalog speaker from Manila, explained in natural Tagalog (the everyday written
Filipino of a good teacher: clear, simple, with the English grammar word in brackets the first time,
since that is what the learner will meet in English: *pandiwa (verb)*). From zero to everyday
conversation (A2, touching B1).

## What is hard for a Tagalog speaker, and so what gets the time

- **Tense, not aspect.** Tagalog asks "has it started? is it finished?"; English asks "when?".
  *Kumakain* is both *I eat* (habit) and *I'm eating* (now); *kumain na ako* is *I ate* and *I've eaten*.
  Present simple (13–15) vs continuous (17); the past (24–28); *going to / will* (29, 30); the present
  perfect, which has no Tagalog twin (34–36).
- ***Siya* is both *he* and *she*** (and *niya* both *his* and *her*): taught in lesson 3 and back as a
  mistake in almost every later unit, because it is the slip Filipinos make longest.
- ***a / the*, and plurals.** Tagalog has no articles and *mga* is often left out (*dalawang libro*);
  English needs *a/an* (4), *the* (6, 9), and *-s* even after a number (7).
- ***be* must be said** (*She's a nurse*, not *She nurse*): lessons 1–5, and with feelings (33).
- **The subject comes first.** Tagalog puts the important word first (*Guro siya*); English always
  starts with who or what (1, 3, 13), and questions turn round with *do / does / did* (15, 16, 26),
  never just *ba* added to a statement.
- **Third-person -s** (*She works*): 14, back in 15, 16, 34.
- **One *sa*, many prepositions**: *in / on / at / under / next to* for places (11), *at / on / in* for
  time (22), *to* and no *to* (*go home*) (21).
- ***Gusto* = like and want**: *I like coffee* vs *I want / I'd like a coffee* (18).
- **Philippine English habits** that Americans don't say, shown as mistakes where they belong: *open
  the light* (38), *borrow me your pen* (19), *I'll go ahead* (30), *same with me* (12), *until now* for
  *still* (36), *informations, advices* (37), *I'm boring* for *I'm bored* (33), *…, isn't it?* for
  *di ba* (39), *Yes* to agree with a negative question (39).
- **Politeness without *po*** (2): *please, could you, sir / ma'am*, tone.

## Units and lessons

Levels: lessons 1–19 A1, 20–38 A2, 39–48 B1 (19 / 19 / 10). Unit titles are in Tagalog.

### Unit 1: Mga unang usapan (First conversations, A1)
| # | id | capability | grammar | level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `introduce-yourself` | Introduce yourself | *I am / I'm, you are*; the subject comes first | A1 |
| 2 | `be-polite-without-po` | Be polite in English (there is no *po*) | *please, thank you, excuse me, could you, sir / ma'am* | A1 |
| 3 | `talk-about-him-and-her` | Talk about someone: *he* or *she* | *he / she / it is*; *siya* → two words | A1 |
| 4 | `say-what-someone-is` | Say what someone does and what they're like | *a / an* with jobs; *be* + adjective | A1 |
| 5 | `ask-and-answer-with-be` | Ask yes-or-no questions with *be* and answer | *Are you…? Is she…?*; *Yes, I am. No, I'm not.*; *not* | A1 |
| 6 | `point-and-name-things` | Point at things and name them | *this / that / these / those*; *it*; *What's this?*; *the* | A1 |

### Unit 2: Mga tao, bagay at lugar (People, things and places, A1)
| 7 | `talk-about-more-than-one` | Talk about more than one | plural *-s / -es*, irregular plurals; *-s* after numbers; *we / they* | A1 |
| 8 | `say-what-you-have` | Say what you have | *have / has*; *I don't have* (as a chunk) | A1 |
| 9 | `say-there-is` | Say what there is | *there is / there are*; *Is there…?*; *may* ≠ *have* | A1 |
| 10 | `say-whose-it-is` | Say whose something is | *my, your, his, her, our, their*; *'s*; *mine, yours* | A1 |
| 11 | `say-where-things-are` | Say where people and things are | *in, on, at, under, next to, between*; *Where is…?* | A1 |
| 12 | `describe-people-and-things` | Describe people and things | adjective before the noun, never plural; *very, so, really*; *me too* | A1 |

### Unit 3: Ang araw-araw (Every day, A1)
| 13 | `talk-about-your-day` | Talk about your daily routine | present simple (*I work, I live*); *every day*; time words | A1 |
| 14 | `talk-about-someone-elses-day` | Talk about someone else's routine | third-person *-s* (*she works, he has, she goes*) | A1 |
| 15 | `ask-questions-with-do` | Ask yes-or-no questions about habits | *Do you…? Does she…?*; *don't / doesn't* | A1 |
| 16 | `ask-where-when-why` | Ask where, when, what and why | question words + *do / does*; *What time…?* | A1 |
| 17 | `say-what-youre-doing-now` | Say what you're doing right now | present continuous vs present simple (*kumakain* = both) | A1 |
| 18 | `say-what-you-like-and-want` | Say what you like and what you want | *like* vs *want* vs *would like* (*gusto* = all three) | A1 |
| 19 | `say-what-you-can-do` | Say what you can do, and ask to borrow | *can / can't*; *Can I…?*; *borrow* vs *lend* | A1 |

### Unit 4: Sa labas (Out and about, A2)
| 20 | `ask-for-things-and-help` | Order, shop and ask for help | *Can I have…? Could you…? I'd like…*; *How much is / are…?* | A2 |
| 21 | `tell-people-what-to-do` | Give directions and instructions | imperative; *don't*; *let's*; *go home* (no *to*) | A2 |
| 22 | `talk-about-times-and-dates` | Say the time, the day and the date | *at / on / in* for time; *It's 3:15* | A2 |
| 23 | `say-what-you-have-to-do` | Say what you have to and should do | *have to, need to, should, must*; *don't have to* | A2 |

### Unit 5: Pagkukuwento ng nangyari (Telling what happened, A2)
| 24 | `say-what-you-did` | Say what you did yesterday | past simple, regular *-ed* (and how it sounds) | A2 |
| 25 | `use-common-irregular-pasts` | Use the everyday irregular pasts | *went, ate, saw, had, got, bought, came, took, made* | A2 |
| 26 | `ask-about-the-past` | Ask about the past and say what you didn't do | *Did you…? I didn't* + the plain verb | A2 |
| 27 | `say-where-you-were` | Say where you were and how it was | *was / were*; *there was*; *I was born in* | A2 |
| 28 | `tell-what-happened-in-order` | Tell what happened, in order | *first, then, after that, before, when* | A2 |

### Unit 6: Mga plano at paghahambing (Plans and comparing, A2)
| 29 | `talk-about-your-plans` | Talk about your plans | *going to*; present continuous for arrangements | A2 |
| 30 | `make-promises-and-predictions` | Offer, promise and predict | *will / 'll / won't* | A2 |
| 31 | `invite-and-suggest` | Invite and suggest, accept and say no | *Do you want to…? Let's… Why don't we…? How about…?* | A2 |
| 32 | `compare-things` | Compare people and things | *-er / more, -est / most, as … as*; never *more bigger* | A2 |
| 33 | `say-how-you-feel` | Say how you feel | *be* + feeling; *bored / boring* (*naiinip / nakakainip*) | A2 |

### Unit 7: Mga karanasan (Experiences, A2)
| 34 | `talk-about-experiences` | Talk about what you've done in your life | present perfect, *ever / never* (*nakapunta na*) | A2 |
| 35 | `say-how-long` | Say how long | present perfect + *for / since* (not present + *since*) | A2 |
| 36 | `say-what-youve-just-done` | Say what you've just done, and not yet | *just, already, yet, still* | A2 |
| 37 | `talk-about-how-much-and-how-many` | Talk about how much and how many | countable and uncountable; *much / many, some / any, a lot of* | A2 |
| 38 | `use-everyday-phrasal-verbs` | Use the everyday two-word verbs | *turn on / off, pick up, look for, get up, find out* | A2 |

### Unit 8: Mga kuwento, opinyon at imahinasyon (Stories, opinions and imagination, B1)
| 39 | `answer-yes-and-no-the-english-way` | Answer negative questions and check what you think | *No, I'm not*; *right?* and tag questions (*di ba*) | B1 |
| 40 | `describe-with-who-and-that` | Say which one: "the woman who…" | relative clauses *who, which, that, where* | B1 |
| 41 | `talk-about-how-things-used-to-be` | Say how things used to be | *used to* (*dati*) | B1 |
| 42 | `set-the-scene-in-a-story` | Set the scene in a story | past continuous + *when / while* | B1 |
| 43 | `talk-about-real-possibilities` | Talk about what will happen if… | first conditional (*if* + present, *will*) | B1 |
| 44 | `imagine-and-give-advice` | Imagine, and give advice | second conditional; *If I were you* (*kung ako sa iyo*) | B1 |
| 45 | `report-what-people-said` | Pass on what someone said | *said (that), told me (to)*; *daw* | B1 |
| 46 | `give-your-opinion` | Give your opinion, agree and disagree | *I think, I agree, I'm not sure, it seems* | B1 |
| 47 | `understand-the-passive` | Understand the passive | *is made, was built*; object focus ≠ passive | B1 |
| 48 | `keep-a-conversation-going` | Keep a conversation going | follow-up questions, reactions; *How about you?* | B1 |
