# English → Chinese (en-zh): the plan

Mandarin Chinese as the basics course writes it (mainland standard, simplified characters), taught to an
English speaker who knows no grammar. Every Chinese text has its pinyin (`"reading": "pinyin"`).

## What makes Chinese Chinese, and the order that follows from it

Chinese verbs never change: no "I am / he is", no "go / went". So the grammar is **word order** and a few
**little words**. A beginner can say a great deal in the first hour (我是…, 我要…, 多少钱?), and the hard
parts come later, when they want to say *how* things happened: the aspect words 了, 过, 在, 着, and the
words after a verb that say its result (听懂, 看完, 拿出来).

The order of this course, as a teacher of Chinese would choose it:

1. **Talk first** (unit 1): 是 for "am / is / are" with nouns, but **not** with adjectives (我很忙, not
   我是忙): this is the first big surprise and comes back often. 吗 questions, then question words that
   stay where the answer goes (你叫什么?).
2. **Measure words early** (unit 2), because you can't count, point (这本书) or order (一杯咖啡) without
   them. Shopping is the first mission.
3. **的, 有, 在** (unit 3): whose; have and "there is" (both 有, never 不有); where things are (在 + the
   place, with 上/里 after the noun).
4. **Word order for a whole day** (unit 4): who + when + where + does what (我明天在家看书); verbs in a
   row (去超市买东西); 喜欢, 会/能/可以, plans, polite requests.
5. **Aspect, a spiral** (unit 5): 了 for something done, 了 for a change, 在 for now, 过 for ever, then
   how long. 不 vs 没 comes back with each.
6. **Comparing and describing** (unit 6): 比 (and no 很 in it), 一样 and 最, 得 for how well, 有点儿 /
   一点儿, and the describing phrase before the noun (我昨天买的书).
7. **Getting things done** (unit 7): the complements (results, can/can't manage, directions), advice and
   rules, "about to".
8. **Connecting ideas** (unit 8): 因为…所以, 虽然…但是 (both halves!), 如果…就, 以后/的时候 (after the
   clause), opinions.
9. **Stories and conversation** (unit 9, B1): 是…的, 把, 被, 着, telling a story with 就/才, 越来越,
   passing on a message, wishes, and the little words that keep a conversation going (呢, 吧, 啊).

## The spiral: where each big structure comes back (practice with `from`)

- **是 and the missing 是**: 1 (I am a…) → 2 (是吗? 不是) → 3 (no 是 with adjectives) → 8 (no 是 with
  prices) → 10 (no 是 with ages) → 25 (no 很 after 比) → 40 (是…的).
- **Questions**: 2 (吗) → 4 (question words in place) → 16 (还是) → 19 (A不A) → 27 (得怎么样) → 35 (为什么)
  → 46 (passing on a question: 他问我去不去) → 48 (呢).
- **Measure words**: 5 → 6 → 7 (一杯) → 8 (prices) → 10 (几口人) → 29 (那本书) → 43 (一张画).
- **的**: 9 (whose) → 29 (describing phrase) → 40 (是…的, a different job).
- **有**: 10 (have) → 12 (there is) → 43 (墙上挂着…, a scene).
- **在**: 11 (where it is) → 14 (where you do it) → 22 (in progress) → 41 (把…放在).
- **Word order (who, when, where, does what)**: 13 → 14 → 15 → 20 (昨天…了) → 38 (以后 after the clause).
- **想 / 要**: 7 (want a thing) → 18 (plans) → 33 (要: must) → 34 (要…了).
- **会 / 能 / 可以**: 17 → 19 (可以…吗) → 31 (听不懂 vs 不能) → 33 (不可以).
- **了 and the other aspect words**: 20 (done) → 21 (change) → 22 (在) → 23 (过) → 24 (how long) → 34
  (要…了) → 40 (是…的, not 了) → 43 (着) → 44 (a story).
- **不 and 没**: 2–3 (不) → 10 (没有) → 20 (没 for the past) → 23 (没…过) → 30 (没听懂) → 31 (听不懂).
- **Complements after the verb**: 27 (得) → 30 (results) → 31 (can / can't) → 32 (directions) → 41 (把)
  → 42 (被).
- **Comparing**: 25 → 26 → 45 (比以前, 越来越).
- **Linking**: 15 (先…然后) → 35 → 36 → 37 → 38 → 39 (because, in an opinion) → 44 (a story).

## Conventions

- **Pinyin**: tone marks, words joined as a dictionary writes them (Zhōngguó, shénme, xuésheng), a capital
  at the start of a sentence and on names. Neutral tones unmarked (ma, de, le, ge, men). 一 and 不 are
  written with the tone they are said with (bú shì, yí ge, yìqǐ), as Chinese textbooks do; other tone
  changes (nǐ hǎo said "ní hǎo") are left to the tip in lesson 1. `tests/js/grammar-zh.test.mjs` checks
  every syllable against CC-CEDICT.
- **Northern standard forms** as the basics course: 一点儿, 有点儿, 哪儿, 这儿, 那儿 (with a note that
  the south says 一点, 哪里, 这里).
- **Numbers in characters** in examples (二十五块), so every character has its pinyin.
- Practice options are characters only (the format has no readings there): every `why` gives the
  pinyin of the right answer, and every mistake's `why` gives the right sentence with its reading.
