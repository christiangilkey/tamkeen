Privacy Policy

Tamkeen for Netflix (browser extension) · Last updated October 24, 2026

"Tamkeen for Netflix" is a free browser extension for learning a language. On Netflix's website it shows the subtitles in the language you are learning as words you can click, with the whole transcript beside the video and the meaning of any word you click. It works together with the free Tamkeen web app (christiangilkey.github.io/tamkeen/app), where your library and saved words live.

In short: the extension keeps its data in your own browser. It sends nothing to the developer, has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and never sells or shares your data. The only things that leave your browser are listed below, and each one is needed for a feature you use.

Tamkeen and this extension are not made, endorsed or sponsored by Netflix. "Netflix" is a trademark of Netflix, Inc.; it is named here only to say which website the extension works with.

Where the extension runs

  • Netflix's website (www.netflix.com), where you watch with your own Netflix account.
  • Netflix's video servers (*.nflxvideo.net), only to recognise and read the subtitle files Netflix's player downloads.
  • The Tamkeen web app's own pages (christiangilkey.github.io/tamkeen/app/), to pass your settings and saved words between the web app and the extension.

It does not run on any other website, and it does not look at your browsing anywhere else.

What it does on Netflix's pages

  • The player. It reads where the video is (the time, playing or paused, the speed, the title's number) and controls it when you use Tamkeen's buttons (play, pause, jump back or ahead, change the speed, jump to a line you click). It picks the soundtrack and the subtitles in the language you are learning. Netflix remembers that subtitle choice for your Netflix profile, as it would if you chose it yourself.
  • The screen. While a title plays it hides Netflix's own subtitles and controls and fits the picture beside Tamkeen's subtitle bar and transcript.
  • The subtitles. It watches the network requests made in Netflix tabs only to notice when Netflix's player downloads a subtitle file. It then downloads that same file once more from Netflix's servers (without your Netflix cookies) and reads its lines. A file in another language is dropped as soon as its language is known. Nothing else about those requests is read or kept.
  • The title's name. It asks Netflix's own website for the name of the title you are watching (the same information Netflix's page shows), so the transcript and your saved words can say which title they came from.

It never reads or stores your Netflix password, your account or payment details, your profiles, or your viewing history. It does not download or copy videos, and it does not get around Netflix's sign-in, subscription or copy protection: you need your own Netflix subscription, and the video plays in Netflix's own player.

What it keeps in your browser

All of this stays in your browser's storage for the extension, on your computer. Removing the extension deletes it.

The settings it receives never include your Google account, your email address, sign-in tokens or any other keys: the web app hands over only the items listed above.

What leaves your browser, and to whom

  • The Tamkeen web app, in the same browser. When you open the web app, the extension hands it the words you saved and the titles you watched (with their subtitle lines), and the web app hands the extension the settings above. This happens inside your browser, between the web app's page and the extension; nothing goes to a server. The web app then keeps them in your library on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you turned on sync there. What the web app does is described in Tamkeen's privacy policy.
  • The AI provider you chose, only if you use AI explanations. When you click a word and Tamkeen asks an AI to explain it, the extension sends, straight from your browser to that provider (Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI or xAI Grok) with your own key: the word, its subtitle sentence and the sentences before and after it, the name of the title and that it is from Netflix, and the language you want the explanation in. Nothing else about you is sent. That provider's privacy policy applies to these requests. With "No AI" chosen in Tamkeen's settings, words are explained from the downloaded dictionary and nothing is sent to any AI provider.
  • GitHub Pages, to download dictionaries. If you agree to download the dictionary for your language, the files come from Tamkeen's website (christiangilkey.github.io/tamkeen/lexicon/), hosted by GitHub. That is an ordinary download: no personal data is sent, though GitHub, like any website, sees your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Netflix. Only the requests described above: the title's name, and the subtitle file downloaded again. Netflix's own privacy policy covers your use of Netflix.

The extension itself never contacts the developer, Tamkeen's go-between server, the Friends service (Supabase) or Google Drive. Those are features of the Tamkeen web app, used only if you turn them on there, and are covered by Tamkeen's privacy policy.

Netflix's subtitles

Subtitles are read only to show them to you as words to click and to keep the transcript in your own Tamkeen library, for your own study. They are never published, never sent to the developer and cannot be sent to friends from Tamkeen.

What the developer does not do

  • No selling or renting of your data, to anyone.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No use of your data for anything other than the extension's purpose (clickable subtitles for learning a language on Netflix), and no use or transfer of it to decide creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No code loaded from the internet: everything the extension runs is inside the extension you install.

The use of information received by this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Use "No AI" in Tamkeen's settings to keep word lookups entirely in your browser.
  • Remove an AI key in Tamkeen's settings; the next time you open the web app, the extension gets the change.
  • Remove the extension (in your browser's extensions page) to delete everything it keeps.

Children

The extension does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page, with a new date at the top. For questions, open an issue at github.com/christiangilkey/tamkeen/issues. The extension's code is public in the same place.
