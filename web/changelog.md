# Tamkeen changelog

What changed in each version of Tamkeen, in plain words. The newest version is at the top.

- **Unreleased** lists changes already made but not yet in a released version.
- When a version is released, its Unreleased notes move under a new heading for that version.
- Each line starts with the kind of change: **New**, **Improved**, **Changed**, **Fixed**, **Removed** or **Security**.
- A note in brackets, such as *(Android)*, says which versions a change is for. A line with no note applies to every version that existed at the time.
- The four versions are the **desktop app** (Windows, Mac, Linux), **Android**, **iPhone** and the **web app**. "The phones" means Android and iPhone.

The app was called **Arabic Podcast Studio** until v0.8.0.

## Unreleased

## v0.68.0 — 2026-11-22

- **Changed:** The word popup's **?** ("Not known yet", which kept a word amber without saving it) is now **Important**, an exclamation mark **!**. Tapping it marks the word's card important, so it comes first in reviews and among new words; on a word not saved yet it saves it (with the meaning chosen among the bubbles and the folder button's folder, as Save does) and marks it. Tapping it again takes the mark off. "Not known yet" is gone everywhere: words marked that way before are no longer amber and show as new (purple) words again; the old marks are left stored, untouched.
- **Improved:** Vocab: Important words show in **yellow** (a dark gold on the light theme, so they stay easy to read), beside their flag, and a new **!** button next to the folder button shows only the Important words, of the folder that is open or of all of them. It stays on until tapped again.
- **Changed:** The word popup (every player, reels, web pages, books, course words, Conversation, Type what you hear) has a **folder button** beside Save in place of the microphone ("Add audio", which only opened Settings → Card voices; that setting is still there, and each card can still record its own audio). The button shows the folder the word's card goes in; a tap opens the folder list (New folder at the top, then Not in a folder and your folders) to pick another, and the choice is kept for the next word. **Save** saves into that folder with no extra question. For a word you have already, Save says Saved, the button shows the folders its card is in, and the list puts it in another folder or takes it out ("Make a second card" is there if you want one). On a phone the button row stays on one line, with the folder's name cut short. The Netflix and Prime Video extension's popup has no folder button (its words go to Tamkeen without a folder, as before).
- **Changed:** The word popup shows the word's other meanings as **bubbles** beside the definition (under it on a phone; to its left in a right-to-left language), one for each meaning that really differs, such as *tiempo*: time, tense, weather. The chosen one is lit; tapping another shows that meaning, and **Save keeps the chosen bubble's meaning**, with no "Which meaning?" question after Save any more. With an AI set up, its meaning for the sentence comes first and is chosen to start with, the dictionary's after it; with no AI, the dictionary's first meaning. A word with one meaning shows no bubbles. (Add words keeps its own "Which meaning?" screen.)
- **Changed:** A card keeps the **short meaning**: *perro* saved "dog", not "dog (the species Canis familiaris …)", from the popup's bubbles and from Add words' "Which meaning?" alike. The fuller wording goes in the card's notes. Choosing a word's first meaning in Add words no longer makes its sentence again.
- **Fixed:** A meaning starting with "letter", such as *carta*'s "letter (document)", was left out of a word's meanings as if it named a letter of the alphabet.
- **Fixed:** The word popup's Save did nothing when the lookup had failed (no internet, say); it saves the word without a meaning again, as it used to.
- **Changed:** **New folder** is at the top of every folder list instead of the bottom: the Vocab page's folder sheet, its put-in-a-folder menu, a word's folders, Add words' Folder choice and its "put it in another folder" box.
- **Fixed:** Web app → YouTube: each video's picture sat wherever the length of its title put it, so no two rows lined up. The picture is now at the start of the row (the right in right-to-left languages) at one width, with the title and channel beside it.
- **Fixed:** Library: the covers flickered while something was being transcribed. Each progress update of a transcription (of another podcast's episode, or one further down the list) redrew the whole list about once a second, and every picture loaded again. Now only a real change of state counts, and a redraw keeps the cards and pictures already showing.
- **Fixed:** Library: a web page or book read to the end said the command "Read" in other languages (Spanish "Leer", Arabic "اقرأ", German "Lesen") in the Filter, the buttons under the heading and its label; it now says it was read ("Leído", "تمت القراءة", "Gelesen").
- **Improved:** Type what you hear (every player and the courses): once what was said is shown, a **Hide** button at the end of the Hear it again / Slower row puts it away again, with what you typed back in the box to listen again and change it; the check mark shows it again. On a phone it is an eye struck through, so the row stays on one line in every language.
- **Improved:** New words show in purple as soon as a transcript, page or reel opens, instead of after a wait. The word dictionaries are read while the app is idle after it starts, the words on screen are coloured first and the rest a piece at a time, and a YouTube video no longer holds the colours back for up to 5 seconds while its player loads. An hour-long Arabic transcript went from about half a second (plus that wait) to under 30 ms on a computer.
- **Improved:** Library: under each tab's heading, one-tap buttons pick what to show, with how many of each: **All / Listened to / Not listened to** (Watched / Not watched for videos, Read / Not read for pages), and on the tabs that are transcribed **All / Transcribed / Not transcribed**. They do what the Filter button's popup does (which now has Not transcribed too), and the two always agree.
- **Improved:** Add → Podcast: the box at the top of Trending now takes a podcast's name as well as its feed address. Type a name ("فنجان", "The Daily") and press Search: a list of shows with their cover, name and maker comes up, from Apple Podcasts' public directory, with shows in the language you're learning first; + Add puts one in your library just as a pasted feed does. A feed address still adds the podcast at once.
- **Changed:** Library → Conversation no longer has a line of text at the top ("Practise speaking with someone who never tires…"): the situations start straight away.

## v0.67.0 — 2026-11-21

- **New:** *(desktop app and web app on a computer, in Chrome or Edge)* **Prime Video with clickable subtitles.** The browser extension is now called **Tamkeen for Netflix & Prime Video**. On Prime Video's website, a panel beside the player shows the subtitle line on screen as words you can tap for their meaning and save to Vocab, with where it came from ("Prime Video · the show · S1:E3"), and lists the lines already shown. It only reads the subtitle line already on the screen: it never controls Amazon's player, changes nothing on Amazon's page, asks Amazon for nothing and downloads no video, sound or subtitle files. Turn the subtitles on in Prime Video's own menu. The Library's Netflix tab and the + sheet's Netflix step now have a **Get the Tamkeen extension** button (**Open Prime Video** once it is installed); on a phone they say it works on a computer.
- **Changed:** **Every Library tab now looks like YouTube**: big pictures, with what you are in the middle of at the top, then what you haven't started (newest added first), then what you have finished at the bottom. "Finished" means played to the end, a web page 70% read or a book's last page. A **Filter** button on every tab chooses what to show (in progress, new, finished) and the order (newest or oldest added, shortest, longest), and each tab remembers its choice; the transcription filters moved into it too. On the YouTube tabs, the video in the middle of the screen plays silently in YouTube's own player, one at a time (not on mobile data or with Save data on). Long lists draw 60 at a time with **Load more**, and Tamkeen now remembers where you were in your last 500 items instead of 40.
- **Improved:** **Saving a word with no AI asks which meaning the card is for** when the dictionary's meanings really differ (Spanish *tiempo*: time, tense or weather), in the word popup and in Ask by voice. A word with one meaning is saved straight away, as before.
- **Improved:** **Ask by voice understands questions in your own language**, in all 16 of the app's languages ("¿cómo se dice perro en árabe?", "ما معنى bonito", "añádelo" to add the card), and its examples are in the app's language too. With an AI set up, nothing new is downloaded. With no AI, the first such question downloads your own language's dictionary once (1 to 10 MB, only yours; nothing new in English). Bisaya by voice on the computer is heard well only with the Bisaya speech model from **Settings → Model**; typing a Bisaya question always works.
- **Improved:** *(desktop app and web app on a computer)* In right-to-left languages (Arabic, Persian), the Library's **+** button now sits at the bottom left, so it no longer covers the side list (which is on the right in those languages). The arrow and the **Click here to add things** note turn round with it and point at it from the page side. Phones, narrow windows and left-to-right languages keep it at the bottom right.
- **Improved:** **While Type what you hear is open, the player's Play button works like the check mark.** Press it once to see what was said, and again to hear the next piece, which stops at its end like the others. The Space key, a controller's play button and the keyboard's or phone's play key do the same, in every player with the window (podcasts, audiobooks, YouTube, Netflix, your own audio and videos, reels). Pause still pauses; with the window closed, Play plays straight on as before. (YouTube's own play button, inside its video, is YouTube's and plays on.)
- **Fixed:** **Type what you hear now stops a podcast at the end of each piece, as it does on YouTube.** Pressing the forward or back arrow (or starting another piece) while one was still playing used to lose the next piece's end, so it played straight on into the rest of the episode. Now every word, sentence or gist stops right after it ends (within about a hundredth of a second, at normal speed and with Slower). The same goes for audiobooks and your own audio and videos.
- **Improved:** When a Library section is empty, a handwritten note above the teal arrow now says **Tap here to add things** (**Click here to add things** with a mouse), in the arrow's colour and slightly tilted. It fades in with the arrow and turns round with it when the + button is on the left. The handwriting is Caveat (SIL Open Font License), included in the app; languages in other alphabets show the note in the app's usual letters.

## v0.66.0 — 2026-11-20

- **Improved:** *(web app)* When the browser has no voice for the language you're learning, **Settings → Card voices** now says how to add one on the system you're on: on Windows, Settings → Time & language → Speech → Manage voices → Add voices (or add the language with Text-to-speech ticked), with an **Open Windows speech settings** button; on a Mac, System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices; on Android, Install voice data in Speech Services by Google; on an iPhone, Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. Then reopen the browser and press **Check again**. It also says that Microsoft Edge has many voices of its own (desktop Chrome's own voices have no Arabic). The desktop app's Windows steps name the current **Manage voices** menu too.
- **Improved:** *(phones and web app)* **Settings → Card voices** now says plainly that the free "computer" voices are made by the **Tamkeen desktop app**, installed on a Windows, Mac or Linux computer signed in to the same Google Drive. In the web app on a computer, "Your computer makes them" read as that very browser, which can't make them. The option is now called **Tamkeen desktop app**, the waiting box ("Waiting for the desktop app: 4 cards") says what the desktop app needs, that the web app can't make them itself, and that this browser's or phone's own voice reads the cards until they arrive (and for good if no desktop app ever syncs), with a **Get the desktop app** button. The desktop app's own wording ("This computer") is unchanged.
- **New:** *(the creator only)* A **creator bar** at the top of Home: how many people are online now, and the limit nearest to full. A tap opens every limit as "used / limit" with a bar that turns amber from 70% and red from 90%: people online against live connections, active users in the last 30 days, database size and file storage, then the limits only Supabase's Usage page can fill in (data sent out, live messages, function calls), where the space goes, and the free allowances of Tamkeen's other services (GitHub Pages, the Cloudflare go-between, Google Drive, Azure Speech). Counts and sizes only, never anything about a person. Needs `supabase/v14-creator.sql`.
- **Fixed:** In the flashcards, the reward popup is back in the card's empty top corner, just under the timing bar and level with the **New word** badge, where it covers nothing. Since the speed streak's line was added above the card (v0.56.0) it had been landing on that line, the **+10 s** button and the timing bar. Its words are no longer cut off ("+33 …"): where there isn't room for one line, the XP goes on a line of its own, then the letters get smaller, and it keeps clear of the longer badges of other languages. What the speed streak says (**New best speed streak**, **+10 s boost earned**, **Time's up**) now shows in its own line above the card instead of across the word.
- **Fixed:** **Long podcast episodes no longer fail on a phone when the connection drops for a moment** (seen with a 2-hour episode in the web app: "Couldn't reach Groq… (NetworkError when attempting to fetch resource.)"). Phones cut the internet to an app or browser in the background or asleep, more so when the battery is low, and each 8-minute piece used to get four quick tries, then the whole episode failed, and **Retry** began again from the start. Now a piece that can't be sent is tried again for about two minutes ("The connection dropped. Trying again in 20 seconds…"); while the app is in the background or the phone is offline it waits, and carries on by itself when it's back ("Waiting for the internet to come back…"); every piece already done is kept, so **Retry** carries on from where it stopped instead of downloading and paying for the audio again; and the message says where it stopped and what to do (keep Tamkeen open with the screen on, plug the phone in). If a piece still won't go up while the app is open and online (a slow upload: the owner's stopped at 8%, which is where the first 8-minute piece, 7.3 MB, is sent), it is sent in halves instead, down to 1-minute pieces, and the rest of the episode and the next few days' episodes go in the smaller size. While a piece goes up the Library says so ("Sending 0:00–8:00 to Groq…"), so a long upload no longer looks stuck. The web app also keeps the screen on while an episode is being transcribed, where the browser allows it. Phones and web app.
- **Changed:** **Every player's bar now matches YouTube's** (podcasts, audiobooks, your own audio and videos, and Netflix): **Type what you hear** (the keyboard button) sits in the bar beside the speed instead of in the top row, the 5-second buttons are smaller, and the "start of the sentence" button is gone (the R key still does it), so the bar no longer runs off the edge of a phone. The play button stays in the middle. **Type what you hear on a podcast** (and an audiobook, your own audio or videos) **works exactly as on YouTube**: the keyboard button hides every line of the transcript, plays one word, one sentence or a few sentences (the gist) at a time with the same ✓ and back and forward arrows, and after the ✓ shows what was said, each word one tap from the word popup.
- **Fixed:** A podcast, audiobook or your own audio that is still loading no longer says "Playback failed: The fetching process for the media resource was aborted…". Play pressed while the sound is on its way is kept, and it plays as soon as it can; meanwhile a small **Loading…** line shows under the time bar. A sound that really can't be loaded says so there, with **Try again** (and after 20 seconds of loading, that it is taking a long time). Your own videos get the same in-place message and Try again.
- **Changed:** **Type what you hear** no longer marks your answer. After the ✓, what was really said (the word, the sentence or the gist) is shown plainly above what you typed, with nothing in red, nothing crossed out and no score ("0 of 12 words right" and "…% right so far" are gone), so you compare them yourself. Every word of what was said still opens the word popup with one tap, and the minutes still count as listening.
- **Improved:** **YouTube videos with a dubbed track in the language you're learning** (YouTube's multi-language audio): YouTube starts them in their original language, and Tamkeen can't switch the track for you. So when one opens, a box under the video now explains how: tap ⚙ (Settings) in the YouTube player → Audio track → your language. It has **Don't show this again**; after that, the short note under the video says it instead. When the captions are YouTube's translation of the original, the box says they won't match the dubbed sound exactly.
- **Fixed:** **The built-in dictionary gives a translation, not a grammar description.** Many words used to show what form they are as their meaning ("active participle of اِبْتَلَّ (ibtalla)", "plural of مَاء", "post-1990 spelling of abîme", "gerund of comer"). Now the meaning is the translation (مُبْتَلّ "wet", مِيَاه "waters", abime "abyss, chasm", comiendo "eating", casas "houses", mangé "eaten", أَكْبَر "bigger, biggest"), and what form it is shows under **Form** in the word popup and goes in the card's **Grammar notes**. It is taken from the word's other meanings, from the meaning the dictionary gives in brackets, from the other word's meaning made into that form, or from Wiktionary's translation lists. About 28,500 entries in the 17 dictionaries are fixed this way, plus hundreds of thousands of word forms (Spanish, French, Russian, Arabic and others). Cards saved earlier with such a line get the translation the next time they are reviewed or opened, but only when their meaning is exactly the dictionary's line: a meaning you wrote or changed is never touched.
- **Fixed:** Looking up a word spelled like one of the app's own programming names (Spanish *constructor*) no longer fails with an error.
- **Improved:** **"Which meaning?" in Add words asks about real differences only.** Two meanings that are one sense said with two English words no longer make a choice: the dictionaries now list about 21,000 such pairs, taken only where English Wiktionary gives each word as a synonym of the other (Arabic سحر "enchantment" and "magic" are one sense; "time" and "tense" stay two). And a word that can also be read as another word's participle no longer offers that word's bare verb: مبتل offers "wet", "testing, trying" and "afflicting", not "to test, to try"; a gerund's other meanings are gerunds too (comiendo: "eating", "having lunch").
- **Improved:** **Ask by voice** (the microphone beside Add words on the Vocab page) now shows what you can say as soon as it opens, for the language you're learning: "How do I say colorful in Spanish?", "What does bonito mean?" (a real word of that language, in its own script: جميل, きれい, красивый…) and, after an answer, "add it". Tap one to put it in the typing box. They're in English because that's how the question is understood.

## v0.65.0 — 2026-11-20

- **Improved:** **Long YouTube videos start sooner.** YouTube's player now starts loading before the video's transcript is drawn, and the purple "new word" colouring is worked out once the player has loaded, instead of all of it first. Your own videos get the same spinner over the picture, and Netflix one beside **Opening Netflix…**. After 20 seconds the spinner becomes a short message with **Try again**. Pressing play before a YouTube video has loaded now plays it as soon as it can, instead of saying it is still loading.
- **Improved:** **Type what you hear:** once checked, the real sentence (or the gist's full text) can be tapped word by word: one tap opens the usual word popup with its meaning and Save button, as in the transcript and captions. It works in every player that has it (podcasts, audio and your own videos, YouTube, Netflix, reels and the courses), and the right / nearly right / missed marks stay on the words.
- **Changed:** In Vocab, the folder bar (the folder on show, such as **All words** or **Not in a folder**) is now in the app's blue, like the Review card, so it is clear it can be tapped to change folder.
- **Fixed:** **Type what you hear** on YouTube with a phone's keyboard up: Word · Sentence · Gist and the round ✓ button were hidden behind the video (YouTube's video can't be squeezed below 200 px, so it hung over the top of the panel). While typing, the video is now just those 200 px and the line about the captions makes room. In every player the row with the ✓ also stays at the top of the panel while what is under it scrolls, and on a short screen the gist's 2–5 sit on the same line.
- **Fixed:** A lesson made before v0.64.0 showed "No audio in this lesson" even when its PDF or Word file has sound buttons: it now offers **Add the file again to find its sound links**, which puts the spots where a new import would (the lesson's own text and answers stay as they are), and sounds already in the lesson fill the spots they are named for.
- **Fixed:** Finding the questions in a long file of passages: a question in slightly bigger print than the rest of its page is no longer taken for a heading, a line such as "Answers" part-way through no longer turns the rest of the file into the answer key, a file can now have three times as many paragraphs, and a file with more than 200 questions says that only the first 200 were kept instead of dropping the rest silently.
- **Fixed:** In a Word or PDF lesson, a question without a number now counts as a question when it ends with a colon and its options a, b, c… follow ("Two men were executed for:"), as it already did when it ended with a question mark. In the owner's "100 Passages" file, everything from text 33 on had turned into plain text: all 100 texts now have their questions (137 instead of 77). Options with no question line before them become a question for the teacher to word, and an option's last words that Word put on a line of their own join the option.

Add the sound files with the document, with **Add audio** (several at once), **Add a folder**, or by dropping them on the lesson, and each goes to its spot by name ("01.mp3" fills "1.mp3"). Spots go to a class with the lesson.

- **New:** **"Which meaning?" in Add words.** When a word has several really different meanings, one screen before the cards are saved lists each such word with its meanings, **All of these** and **My own meaning**; the everyday meaning is chosen to start with. The 5-second buttons are smaller, and the "start of the sentence" button is gone from YouTube's bar (the R key still does it). The play button stays in the middle.
- **Fixed:** In Safari on a Mac, cards with no recording of their own (most DLI ICPT words) said only the first sound of the word and no sentence, because Safari cuts off speech started straight after stopping the last one. *(web app)*
- **Fixed:** The lesson page's back button showed a round arrow that looked like Refresh; it is now the usual **← My files**.

## v0.63.0 — 2026-11-18

- **New:** **One card per word.** A word you already have is kept once, in its own language, and folders only sort it. In **Add words**, typing a word you have shows a small button saying so and which folders it is in; tap it to put the same card in another folder (or a new one) instead of making a second card (**Add anyway** still can). DLI decks and the study words a teacher gives you use the card you already have, put in the chapter's or the lesson's folder, and it gets the chapter's recording if it had no sound.
- **New:** **Aim your own attacks** in live battles. After a right answer the world stays frozen for a moment while you choose where the move goes: the mouse aims (W A S D or the arrows walk) on a computer, a second finger aims on a phone, and the right stick aims on a controller. Drops land where you point, within their reach.
- **New:** **Answer race** in live battles. When one side attacks, the rival answers too, and the faster right answer wins: out-answered, your attack does half damage, and a quick right answer to the rival's attack dodges it for half damage. "(Rival) answered quicker" shows the right answer and the question closes at once.
- **New:** **More kinds of battle moves:** Body Slam, Slash Through, Grapple Toss, Sky Dive, Burrow, Energy Orb, Cone Wave, Shockwave, Call Down and Focus Beam in the shop, each with its own art, and some older moves get new shapes (Wildfire and Overgrowth as cones, Riptide and Resonance as rings, Pounce as a slash, Charge as a slam). Areas show element marks round their edge instead of a red circle.
- **New:** **Guild tavern.** On Home and the Guilds page, your guild-mates' favourite creatures gather in a tavern by the fire. Arrows switch between your guilds, and a tap on a creature says whose it is.
- **New:** DLI classes: a class's teachers are listed in the class, and a teacher can add another DLI teacher by username, or take out one who joined after them. *(Needs the database update supabase/v13-dli-class-teachers.sql.)*
- **Changed:** The weekly battle rules no longer include the weeks that allowed only one element's creatures, since most players hold only one or two kinds.
- **Changed:** When your AI key is refused while adding words, **Add words** fills the cards in without it (from the built-in dictionary) and a popup says so, with a button to switch to using no key.
- **Fixed:** With no AI key, a word's first meaning was sometimes a rare one, because the built-in dictionary kept Wiktionary's order, which goes by where a word comes from, not how much it is used: Spanish guapo was "arrowroot" before "handsome", Chinese 东西 "east and west" before "thing", Arabic قلم "to cut" before "pen", Bisaya asa "pay attention to" before "where", and a letter's name came before the word (Spanish de, "the letter D", before "of"). The everyday meaning now comes first, worked out from Wiktionary's translation lists (guapo is the word given for "handsome"), in every language and on every device; where those lists can't tell, Wiktionary's order stays. Japanese words Wiktionary has only as a name (かける) now get their meaning from JMdict.
- **Changed:** The **Mnemonic maker** (on the review card, in the word editor and in **Add words**) now offers only the word that sounds like the new one, written as its syllables in capitals, such as **MUR-DER**, with no picture, scene or story to read. **Use** puts just that in Memorization notes. This is the same with your AI and with Tamkeen's own maker, which also no longer writes its picture lines. A sound-alike you write yourself as MUR-DER counts as one word when Tamkeen learns from your notes.
- **New:** **Type what you hear.** Every player with sound has a new keyboard button (or press W on a computer): the words go away and you write down what you hear, three ways. **Word:** one word plays and you type it. **Sentence:** one sentence plays; you type it and the real sentence then appears above what you typed, each word marked right, nearly right or missed (with your extra words crossed out). **Gist:** 2 to 5 sentences play in a row (you choose how many); you write what you heard, the words you caught or what it was about, in any language, and then see everything that was said with the words you caught marked. Next plays the next one. Vowel marks, capitals and punctuation never count against you; accents, the forms of a letter and a one-letter slip count as nearly right; Japanese, Chinese and Thai are checked letter by letter. It starts from where you are, cuts long lines at full stops and pauses (so a jingle is never part of a sentence), has Hear it again (Shift+Enter) and Slower, and shows how much you have got right so far. It is in podcasts, audiobooks, your own audio, recordings and videos, YouTube, Netflix, reels (the phones and the web app: after the last piece, Next reel carries on with the next reel) and every course unit, which plays its 50 words or example sentences.
