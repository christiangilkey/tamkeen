Cookie and Storage Notice

Tamkeen · Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Tamkeen uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and sets no cookies of its own. It keeps your data in your device's storage only so the app works, and sites like YouTube or Google set their own cookies only when you choose to use them. Tamkeen is provided by Caliber Products LLC; see also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What "cookies and storage" means

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Apps and web apps can also save data on your device in other ways, such as the browser's local storage and databases. The law treats these the same way, so this notice covers all of them.

Storage Tamkeen needs to work

These are strictly necessary for features you ask for, so they don't need your consent. They stay on your device (and in your own Google Drive if you turn on sync) and are never used to track you or show ads.

  • Your study data and settings: your library, transcripts, saved words, review history, course progress, creature game, settings and any API keys you enter.
  • Downloads: episodes, audiobooks, dictionaries, word lists, example sentences and voices you download, so they work offline.
  • The app itself: in the web app, a copy of the app's files so it opens quickly and offline.
  • Sign-in: if you sign in, the tokens that keep you signed in to Google Drive sync and to the online features (Friends, chat, guilds). They are removed when you sign out or reset the app.
  • Small reminders: things like which tab you had open, which guides you've seen, and your answer to the age question for the online features.

Cookies from other services, only when you use them

Some features show or open another company's service. That company may set its own cookies, under its own policy, when you use the feature:

  • Google sign-in, when you sign in for Drive sync or Friends (Google's cookie policy).
  • YouTube, when you play a YouTube video in Tamkeen (Google's cookie policy).
  • Instagram, when you open reels, which play from Instagram's own page with your own Instagram login (Instagram's cookie policy).
  • Netflix, when you watch Netflix in the computer app or with the Tamkeen for Netflix extension, on Netflix's own pages with your own account (Netflix's cookie notice).
  • Web pages you open in Tamkeen's built-in browser, which may set their own cookies, as they would in any browser.

If you never use a feature, its service sets nothing. You can clear these cookies by signing out of that service, or in your browser's or device's settings.

Your choices

  • To remove everything Tamkeen keeps on a device, use Settings → Data & updates → Reset Tamkeen, or uninstall the app. In a browser, you can also clear the site's data in the browser's settings.
  • To stop the online features, disconnect in Settings → Account & sync.
  • Blocking the storage Tamkeen needs will stop the app from remembering your words and settings.

Changes and contact

If Tamkeen ever starts using cookies or storage that aren't strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will update this notice first and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Questions: tamkeen.app.help@gmail.com.
