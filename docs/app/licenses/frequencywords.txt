Frequency words 1001-3000 in Basics: film and TV subtitle counts (FrequencyWords)

Tamkeen's Basics courses choose words 1001-3000 of each language ("Frequency words") from a blend weighted
toward spoken language: 70% the word counts of film and TV subtitles, 30% wordfreq's counts (see wordfreq.txt).
The subtitle counts are FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), the 2018
lists counted from the OpenSubtitles 2018 collection (http://www.opensubtitles.org/, as published in OPUS:
P. Lison and J. Tiedemann, "OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large Parallel Corpora from Movie and TV Subtitles",
LREC 2016). Tamkeen reads the "<language>_50k.txt" lists as data only; they are not shipped with the app. The
counts are blended, grouped by dictionary word and checked by hand (tools/course/forms.py, candidates.mjs);
the course's words, meanings and sentences are Tamkeen's own.
