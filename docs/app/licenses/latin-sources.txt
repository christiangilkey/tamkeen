Latin word table, word meanings and the 1000 most common Latin words (optional download)

1. The word forms (docs/lexicon/la-lemmas.json.gz)

   The Latin entries of the English Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), as extracted by
   Wiktextract (https://kaikki.org, Tatu Ylonen, "Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured
   Data", LREC 2022): every word form its inflection tables and "form of" entries list, and the
   dictionary form it belongs to, written with its macrons (puellārum: puella, amāvit: amō). Wiktionary's
   text is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
   (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the GNU Free Documentation License.

2. How common each word is (which of two words a shared form goes to, the table's order, and
   web/js/wordlists/la.js)

   Counted by tools/build_latin.py in public-domain Latin books from Project Gutenberg
   (https://www.gutenberg.org): Caesar, Cicero, Virgil, Ovid, Horace, Catullus, Propertius, Persius,
   Quintilian, Vitruvius, Silius Italicus, books of the Vulgate, Augustine, Saint Patrick, Descartes,
   Newton, and the Latin readers and novels of Appleton, Newman, Goffaux, Avellanus and others. The texts
   are in the public domain; only the counts are kept, never any text, and Gutenberg's header and licence
   are not used. The forms are read with the word table above.

The word table and the common-word list are shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0, with credit
as above. This covers these lists only, not the rest of Tamkeen.

3. Latin meanings for "No AI" (docs/lexicon/la-meanings.json.gz): see wiktionary.txt.

4. Reading Latin text in a photo ("Add words" → Scan): Tesseract's Latin trained data
   (@tesseract.js-data/lat, Apache-2.0): see tesseract.txt.
