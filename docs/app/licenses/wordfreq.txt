Common-word lists (the 1000 most common words of each language Tamkeen teaches)

These lists are taken from wordfreq by Robyn Speer (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), version
3.1.1. Tamkeen keeps the 1000 most common words of each language and leaves out numbers, symbols,
stray single letters and words in another alphabet; nothing else is changed.

    Robyn Speer. (2022). rspeer/wordfreq: v3.0 (v3.0.2). Zenodo.
    https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7199437
