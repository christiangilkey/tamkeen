The free book shelf: public-domain books (optional download, one book at a time)

Tamkeen's Books section offers famous books in each language taught, picked because they are in the
public domain: their authors died long enough ago (or the works are old enough) that nobody owns them,
both in the country they were written in and in the United States. Each book is downloaded only when
the learner adds it (docs/books/<language>/<book>.json.gz, made by tools/build_books.py), and each
names where its text was taken from:

  Project Gutenberg (https://www.gutenberg.org): the public-domain text of the eBook. The Project
  Gutenberg header, licence and trademark have been removed, as that licence allows for text that is
  passed on without the Project Gutenberg name; nothing here claims to be a Project Gutenberg eBook.

  Wikisource (https://wikisource.org): public-domain texts transcribed by Wikisource's volunteers.
  Some Bisaya books are taken from the proofread pages of their scans on the multilingual Wikisource
  (scans of the University of Michigan's Philippine collection, books printed in Cebu in 1904-1924).
  The Korean books are Korean Wikisource's (https://ko.wikisource.org) modern-spelling texts of works of
  1917-1948 whose authors all died by 1950; for the poems it also gives as first printed, only the
  modern spelling is kept.

  Aozora Bunko 青空文庫 (https://www.aozora.gr.jp): Japanese works in the public domain, typed in and
  proofread by Aozora Bunko's volunteers; their furigana are left out (Tamkeen adds its own).

Only the text of each work is kept: headings and paragraphs. Introductions, notes and illustrations
by later editors are left out where they could be told apart.
