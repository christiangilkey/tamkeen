The Arabic word dictionaries (downloaded separately, only by people learning Arabic; they are
not part of this program) are cut-down copies of the CAMeL Lab's morphology databases:

  calima-msa-r13 (Modern Standard Arabic) and calima-egy-r13 (Egyptian Arabic):
      GNU General Public License version 2. Derived from Aramorph 1.2.1 (the Buckwalter Arabic
      Morphological Analyzer; portions (c) 2002 QAMUS LLC and the Trustees of the University of
      Pennsylvania).
  calima-glf-01 (Gulf Arabic) and calima-lev-01 (Levantine Arabic):
      Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

The files, their full licence texts, what was changed and how to rebuild them:
https://github.com/christiangilkey/tamkeen/tree/main/docs/lexicon

The same Standard Arabic and Egyptian databases also give the English glosses of the No AI word
meanings for Arabic (docs/lexicon/ar-camel-meanings.json.gz, made by tools/build_arabic_glosses.py),
asked when Wiktionary doesn't have a word. That file is under the GNU GPL v2, like the databases.
