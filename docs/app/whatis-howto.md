# How to use Tamkeen

Tamkeen (تمكين, "empowerment" in Arabic) helps you learn a language from real material: podcasts, videos, shows, reels, books and web pages in the language you are learning. You tap the words you don't know, save them as flashcards, and review them until you know them, while a creature game turns the studying into play. This page starts with a short guide, then lists everything Tamkeen can do.

## Getting started

### 1. Answer the first questions
The first time you open Tamkeen, it asks which language you speak, which one you are learning and your level. It can also ask for an AI key, with step-by-step help, but you don't need one: Tamkeen's built-in dictionaries, memory tricks and example sentences all work without it. To learn more than one language, tick them in **Settings → Languages I'm learning** and switch between them in the top bar.

### 2. Find something to learn from
Open the **Library** tab. Beginners can start with **Courses**: a Basics course with the 1,000 most common words and a Grammar course that starts from zero. Otherwise, press **+** to add a podcast, a YouTube video, a book, a web page or your own audio or video. Podcasts, videos and audiobooks get a transcript with every word timed to the sound, so the words light up as you hear them.

### 3. Tap the words you don't know
Words you have never looked at are shown in **purple**. Tap one to hear it and see what it means in that sentence, then press **Save** to make it a flashcard, with its sentence and sound. Press and drag across several words to look up a whole phrase. To mark a word you already know, press and hold it, then let go.

### 4. Review your words every day
Your saved words come back as flashcards on a schedule timed to your memory. Each card has two answers, **Remembered** or **Not remembered**: tap, swipe right or left, or use a key or a game controller. Tap the word, meaning or sentence on the back of a card to fix it, and use the **Mnemonic maker** for a memory trick. You can also add words yourself in the **Vocab** tab, by typing them, pasting a list or scanning a photo.

### 5. Grow your creature
Studying earns XP. The egg on **Home** hatches and grows as you look up, save and review words and reach your daily goal. Your creatures can battle, and every move needs a right answer about one of your own words.

### 6. Study with friends (optional, 16 and over)
On the **Friends** tab you can sign in with Google, add friends by username, chat, send each other things to study, battle and join guilds.

### 7. Use it on all your devices
Tamkeen runs on Windows, Mac and Linux computers, Android, iPhone and the web. In **Settings → Sync with Google Drive**, connect your own Google Drive, and your library, words, reviews and game reach all your devices within seconds. Your data is kept in a private folder in your own Drive, never with Tamkeen's developer.

### Help on every screen
Press the round **?** button in the top bar (or the ? key on a computer) and Tamkeen points to each button on the screen and says what it does. The big **Continue** button on **Home** always picks up where you left off.

## Everything Tamkeen can do

### Languages
- **Languages you can learn**: Arabic, Spanish, French, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, English, Italian, Tagalog, Brazilian Portuguese, Catalan, Bisaya (Cebuano), Thai, German, Persian (Farsi), Hindi, Korean and Latin, 18 in all.
- **Languages the app can be shown in**: English, Spanish, Arabic, French, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Italian, Tagalog, Brazilian Portuguese, Catalan, Bisaya (Cebuano), Thai, German, Persian (Farsi), Hindi and Korean, 17 in all (you can learn Latin, but the app isn't shown in it).
- **Arabic dialects**: when you learn Arabic, the courses teach Standard Arabic (MSA) or the Levantine, Egyptian, Gulf, Moroccan or Iraqi dialect, whichever you pick.
- **Learn several languages**: tick the languages you're learning in Settings, and the switch in the top bar moves between them, each showing only its own content and words.
- **Everything follows your language**: the language you pick decides what the speech recognition listens for, what the AI explains, which way the text runs (right to left for Arabic and Persian), its font, and what podcasts, YouTube, news, books and Netflix offer you.
- **Content ready for each language**: every language comes with podcasts, YouTube topics, news sites, free books, free audiobooks (none for Thai yet), a list of its 1,000 most common words and a built-in dictionary.
- **Every form counts as one word**: in Arabic, Spanish, French, Italian, Russian, English, Tagalog and Latin, all forms of a word count as the same word, so "ran" and "running" both count as "run" (Spanish commands with pronouns attached and "vos" forms included).
- **Real words in Japanese, Chinese and Thai**: text written without spaces is split into real dictionary words, so you tap whole words rather than single characters.
- **Arabic vowel marks**: a Show vowels switch puts the vowel marks on transcripts, web pages, reels and review cards, added by your computer and shown on your phone once it syncs.
- **Japanese and Chinese readings**: furigana show above Japanese words and pinyin above Chinese ones, and you choose simplified or traditional Chinese characters.
- **Thai pronunciation guide**: a guide to how each Thai word is said sits above it, and you can turn it off.
- **Russian stress marks**: a switch shows which syllable is stressed, and words written with е or ё are treated as the same.
- **English pronunciation**: the word popup shows the American pronunciation of English words.
- **Latin with or without long marks**: Latin words are recognised whether or not they are written with macrons, and search ignores them.

### Getting around
- **Home**: Home greets you with your daily streak, one big Continue button that picks up where you left off, and your creature's habitat.
- **Five main tabs**: Home, Library, Vocab, Friends and Settings hold everything, with a badge on Friends for new requests.
- **Top bar**: the top bar of every screen has the language switch, a refresh button and a round ? button.
- **Help on every screen**: the ? button outlines each button on the screen in turn with a line on what it does (the ? key does the same on a computer), and in the players an animated fingertip shows how to tap a word or slide across several.
- **First-time guides**: a short guide shows the first time you open Home, the Library, Vocab or Friends, and Settings can show them again.
- **First-run questions**: when you first open Tamkeen it asks the language you speak, the one you're learning, your level and (if you want one) an AI key with step-by-step help, and if you've already started learning, the 1,000 most common words count as already seen.
- **Search**: one search box looks through all your transcripts and pages, ignoring Arabic vowel marks and Latin long marks, and a result opens at its paragraph.
- **Swipe between tabs**: on the phones and in the web app, swipe left or right to move between sections.
- **Fix-it popups**: a problem you can put right yourself (the microphone not allowed, a missing AI key, a sign-in) shows one popup with a button that takes you straight to the fix.
- **Troubleshoot**: when an AI or transcription key fails, a Troubleshoot button lists the likely causes and what to do about each.
- **The same look everywhere**: the app's fonts, menus and popups are its own, so every computer and phone shows the same thing, also offline.

### Learn from real material

**The Library**
- **One place for everything**: the Library holds Courses, Podcasts, YouTube, Netflix, Reels, Audiobooks, Books, My webpages, My videos and My audio, and opens on the kind you looked at last.
- **One + button**: the + button opens straight on the way to add the kind you're looking at, with "All the ways to add" for the full list, and an empty section points you to it.
- **Continue where you left off**: each kind remembers what you were watching, reading or hearing, so you can carry on.
- **Delete selected**: tick items and delete them, whole episodes included, from all your devices at once.
- **Send to a friend**: anything in your Library (podcasts, videos, pages, books, reels) can be sent to a friend, and its transcript goes with it.

**Podcasts**
- **Follow podcasts**: follow any podcast and its new episodes arrive in your Library, on every device (29 Latin podcasts among them).
- **Trending podcasts**: the Discover page lists popular podcasts in the language you're learning, from Apple's charts, with hand-picked shows for languages the charts miss, such as Bisaya.

**YouTube**
- **Open YouTube**: a menu of topics (news, music, comedy, cooking, travel and more) searches YouTube in the language you're learning, and starts on what's popular in a country you choose.
- **Tap a video to study it**: a video you pick in YouTube plays in Tamkeen's own player, with its captions as the transcript and every word tappable.
- **Choose your captions**: show only videos whose subtitles were written by a person, or let a video without them use YouTube's automatic captions or its translation.
- **Captions written by Gemini**: for a public video of up to an hour with no captions at all, your own Gemini key can watch it and write the captions, for a few US cents.
- **Other soundtracks**: the player tells you when a video can also be heard in the language you're learning, and where to choose that soundtrack.
- **Follow channels**: follow a YouTube channel from the player or its page, and the channels you follow are listed under YouTube on all your devices.

**Netflix**
- **Netflix with tappable subtitles**: Open Netflix browses titles in the language you're learning, and a title you pick plays with its subtitles as tappable words and a transcript *(desktop app on Windows, and the web app in Chrome or Edge with the "Tamkeen for Netflix" extension)*.
- **Choose soundtrack and subtitles**: Audio and Subtitles buttons switch Netflix's soundtrack and subtitles (for example Arabic subtitles in Standard Arabic or a dialect), the transcript and look-ups follow your choice, and a change of the language you're learning carries over to Netflix.
- **Netflix titles on your phone**: titles watched on the computer arrive in your phone's Library with their subtitles to read.

**Instagram reels**
- **Reels in your language**: the computer finds popular reels in the language you're learning and turns their speech into words, and they play one after another with the words underneath.
- **Your own Instagram saves**: get reels from your saved Instagram posts, all of them or one collection, and tick the ones you want *(desktop app and phones)*.
- **Random reels**: get a chosen number of random reels from all of Instagram in the language you're learning *(desktop app and phones)*.
- **Add a reel by its link**: in the web app, a public reel can be added from its link *(web app)*.
- **Reel player**: reels show big captions, new words in purple, a pause-or-keep-playing switch while you look words up, and the next or previous reel.

**Audiobooks**
- **Free audiobooks**: LibriVox's free public-domain audiobooks in your language are listed with cover, author, length and chapters, most popular first, with a search.
- **Chapter by chapter**: an audiobook's first chapter is transcribed straight away, and "Next chapter" goes on to the next, which is transcribed while you listen.
- **Your own audiobook files**: add one audiobook file or several (one per chapter becomes one book), including DRM-free M4B files from shops such as Libro.fm.

**Books**
- **Free bookshelf**: about 90 famous public-domain books in every language (16 in Latin), read one full page at a time with every word tappable and their bold, italics and headings kept.
- **Your own books**: upload EPUB, PDF, Kindle, Word or plain-text files and read them the same way.

**Web pages**
- **Import a web page**: paste an address, pick one of your saved sites or choose a news site in your language, and read the page with every word tappable, title included, at its own text size.
- **Built-in web browser**: browse inside the app with a start page, starred sites kept on all your devices, and a round "Import to Tamkeen" button *(desktop app and phones; the web app has a page of popular sites instead)*.
- **Share a page to Tamkeen**: on Android, share a page from another app straight to Tamkeen's web app.

**Your own files**
- **My videos**: add your own video files, which are kept in your Google Drive, transcribed and played with tappable captions over the video.
- **My audio**: add your own audio files (MP3, M4A, M4B, AAC, OGG, Opus, FLAC, WAV, WMA, AMR, WebM, MKA or AIFF), transcribed and played like a podcast.

### Transcription and read-along
- **Speech turned into text**: podcasts, videos, reels and audiobooks are written down with every word timed to the sound.
- **On your own computer**: the desktop app writes transcripts itself, and Settings helps you pick a speech model that suits your computer *(desktop app)*.
- **In the cloud**: with your own Groq or OpenAI key, transcripts are written online, so a computer that can't run a big model, or a phone on its own, can transcribe.
- **Ask your computer from your phone**: your phone or the web app can ask for an episode to be transcribed, and your computer picks the request up.
- **Thai speech model**: Thai is transcribed on the computer by Pathumma, a model trained on Thai by Thailand's national electronics centre, with about a quarter fewer mistakes *(desktop app; the Mac uses the standard model)*.
- **Bisaya speech model**: a speech model made for Bisaya writes much better Bisaya transcripts, on the computer only, since cloud services can't do Bisaya *(desktop app)*.
- **How accurate each model is**: for Thai and Bisaya, Settings shows how the language's own model compares with the cloud model.
- **Progress bars**: while your computer transcribes, two bars show it finding the speech and then writing it down, each with its percentage.
- **Misheard words put right**: when a word lookup spots a word the transcript got wrong, it shows the right word and corrects the transcript, with Undo.
- **Read along**: the player lights up each word as it is said and scrolls the text along with it, and pressing play brings you back to the word being said.
- **Slow down and loop**: play slower, loop a sentence, and hear a line again.
- **Skip back and forward**: jump 5 seconds back or forward, to the previous or next line, or back to the start of the sentence (press again to go one sentence further back).
- **Captions and transcript**: videos show tappable captions over the picture, and a transcript panel slides in beside them or folds away with a handle.

### Word lookup and dictionaries
- **Tap any word**: tap a word in a transcript, caption, page, book or reel to hear just that word, pause the audio, and see what it means in this sentence.
- **Look up a phrase**: press on a word and drag (or hold and slide your finger) across up to 30 words, and the popup explains them as one phrase in its sentences.
- **New words in purple**: words you've never looked at show in purple, and looking at or saving one form of a word marks all its forms as seen.
- **Hold to mark known**: press and hold a purple word and let go to mark it known without opening the popup, with Undo; hold a known word to make it new again.
- **"Not known yet"**: the ? button in the word popup marks a word amber everywhere, in all its forms, without saving it or counting it as known.
- **Word menu**: keep holding a word (or right-click it) for Save, Copy, Play from here, and Mark as seen or new.
- **Explanations by AI**: with your own Claude, Gemini, OpenAI or Grok key, an AI explains the word using the sentences around it, says how much it used, and keeps the answer so the same word isn't asked about twice.
- **Built-in dictionary**: with no AI at all, meanings come from a built-in dictionary (Wiktionary) for every language, with a second Arabic dictionary of about 39,000 Standard and Egyptian words and fuller Bisaya dictionaries.
- **Dictionary first**: by default the dictionary answers first and the AI only when the dictionary has nothing, and the dictionary downloads by itself when it's missing.
- **Where each meaning came from**: a tag at the top right of the popup says where the meaning came from, tapping it changes the source, and if the AI fails the popup offers "Use the dictionary instead".
- **Quick or detailed**: a switch in every player shows either just the meaning or the full explanation.
- **Popup buttons**: the popup plays the word or its whole sentence, carries on playing with Continue, and saves the word as a flashcard with its sentence and sound in one tap.
- **Add audio**: when a saved word wouldn't get a voice by itself, an Add audio button takes you to the voice settings.
- **Vibration**: a light vibration when you tap or save a word, and a small celebration on saving *(the phones; can be turned off)*.

### Vocabulary and flashcard review

**Your words**
- **Vocab page**: all the words you've saved, each showing where it came from, opening as a card with a tap.
- **Folders**: put words in folders (one word can be in several), and add or remove many words at once.
- **Sort, filter and export**: sort your words, filter them by date, Select all, and export exactly the words on screen as a spreadsheet file.

**Flashcards**
- **Review on a schedule**: saved words come back as flashcards on a schedule timed to your memory, answered Remembered or Not remembered by a tap, a swipe right or left, a key or a controller.
- **The front never gives it away**: the front of a card never shows the word being asked, even if the meaning mentions it.
- **Meaning and translation**: the back shows the word with its meaning under it, and the sentence with its translation under it, in your own language.
- **Fix a card as you go**: tap the word, meaning, sentence or translation on the back to change it right there (or Copy it), and the change reaches your other devices.
- **Highlight the right words**: hold a word in the card's sentence to mark it as part of what you're learning (a two-part verb, a whole phrase), or take a wrong highlight away.
- **Notes**: each card has Grammar notes and Memorization notes, with a Paste button.
- **Say it**: record yourself saying the word, and hear the real sentence, a short pause, then your recording to compare.
- **Undo and delete**: undo an answer, or delete a word from its card.
- **Review timers**: a time bar on each card, a clock for the whole session and an estimate of the time left help you keep going.
- **Daily goal bar**: a bar under the Review box shows today's new words and reviews done and about how long the rest will take, and tapping it sets how many new words you want a day.
- **Flashcard streak**: a gold flame shows how many days in a row you met your flashcard goal, on Home and beside your name for friends.
- **Cards left on the app icon**: the number of cards left today shows on the app's icon (the taskbar or Dock on a computer, a quiet notification and a home-screen widget on Android, the icon on iPhone, and the web app once added to the home screen).

**Anki and planning**
- **Export for Anki**: make a ready-to-import Anki deck with each word's audio and its review progress, a deck for each language, with card fronts that never give the word away.
- **Two-way Anki sync**: keep your words and reviews in step with Anki on the computer or AnkiDroid on Android *(desktop app and Android; off until you turn it on)*.
- **Anki reviews count in the game**: an Anki add-on lets reviews done in Anki on the same computer count in the creature game *(desktop app)*.
- **Plan today's review**: put today's review in Google Calendar at a time you choose, or send it as a to-do to Google Tasks or Onyx Calendar, with how long it should take.

### Making cards

**Add words**
- **Add words**: type new words one by one, or paste a list of up to 100 (straight from a spreadsheet too), and Tamkeen makes cards of them.
- **Filled in for you**: Tamkeen can add each word's meaning (from the dictionary or your AI), a short example sentence with its translation, a voice and a memory trick, never replaces what you typed, points out words you already have, and lets you look it all over before adding.
- **Your own audio**: record or choose your own audio for any card instead.
- **Scan words from a photo**: photograph a word list, a page of a book or a single word (or choose or paste a picture), and Tamkeen reads the words and their meanings into new cards.
- **Scanning with or without AI**: your AI reads print and handwriting, and without AI or offline the photo is read on the device itself, best with printed text, and photos are never kept.

**Mnemonics**
- **Mnemonic maker**: on the back of a card, the Mnemonic maker suggests words that sound like the word you're learning, each with a picture line that ties it to the meaning.
- **How the word is really said**: with an AI key, it first shows how the word is said, in syllables with the stressed one in capitals, then checks each suggestion really sounds like it.
- **Close or partial, and fits the meaning**: each suggestion says whether it's a close or partial sound match, ones that also point to the meaning come first, and long words get keywords for each part or of two or three words.
- **Use or thumbs down**: Use adds a keyword and its picture to Memorization notes, and thumbs down drops it so it's never suggested for that word again.
- **Mnemonics without AI**: with no AI key, the Mnemonic maker finds sound-alike words from a pronunciation dictionary, in English or in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian or Catalan when the app is shown in one of those.
- **It learns your taste**: the free Mnemonic maker learns what kinds of keywords you like from Use and thumbs down, on all your devices, and Settings can make it forget.
- **It learns from your own mnemonics**: write the sound-alike in CAPITALS in Memorization notes ("a duck sitting on a POT") and, if it really sounds like the word, the Mnemonic maker learns from it, offers it again for other words that sound alike ("You've used this before"), and shows your AI a few of your notes as examples of your style.
- **Mnemonic settings**: choose the Mnemonic maker's AI model and how hard it thinks, with its cost shown (about 2 US cents a press at most).

**Example sentences and card voices**
- **Natural example sentences**: with an AI key, example sentences are written the way a native speaker really talks, in the word's own register (slang stays slang).
- **Real sentences without AI**: with no AI key, Tamkeen finds a real sentence that uses the word, with its translation, preferring one whose other words you already know.
- **Dialect sentences**: when you study an Arabic dialect, example sentences and mnemonic sounds come from that dialect where there are any, and Bisaya has about 20,000 extra sentences from a Cebuano dictionary.
- **Card voices**: cards without their own recording are read out by your device's own voice (free), natural Azure voices with your own key (Arabic by country), or voices made on your computer that reach your phone when it syncs.
- **A voice the moment you save**: with Azure, a word saved from YouTube, Netflix, a reel, a page or a book gets its voice straight away, saying the word and its whole sentence.
- **Latin card voice**: Latin cards are read by an Italian voice using the Church pronunciation, which you can turn off.

### Courses
- **Courses in the Library**: Library → Courses holds a Grammar course and a Basics course for the language you're learning (every language but Latin).
- **Basics**: the 1,000 most common words of the language, 50 at a time, each with an everyday sentence, its translation and audio of both.
- **Add to your cards**: add one basics word, or a whole unit, to your cards in one tap, with its audio, and it goes into your reviews.
- **Grammar from zero**: 45 to 54 lessons for people who know no grammar, each named after something you'll be able to do ("Say what you want", "Tell a story"), from the very beginning to everyday conversation.
- **How a lesson works**: each lesson opens with the sentence you'll be able to say and a short conversation, explains the grammar in plain words with examples and common mistakes, and ends with practice.
- **Many kinds of practice**: multiple choice, fill the gap, put the words in order, choose your replies in a conversation, say it aloud, and "your turn", which your AI can check.
- **Mixed review**: from the fourth lesson some questions come back from earlier lessons, and Mixed review asks questions from all the lessons you've done.
- **Hear every example**: tap any example in a lesson to hear it in your card voice.
- **Readings in Latin letters**: in Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Hindi, Persian, Russian and Arabic, every example shows how it is said in Latin letters, with "Hide readings" to switch it off.
- **Grammar for learning English**: English grammar courses explained in your own language, for speakers of each of the 16 other languages Tamkeen is shown in, in American English.
- **Explained in your language**: courses are explained in English or the language you're learning, and for any other pair your AI can translate a lesson's explanations into your language.
- **Arabic dialect courses**: a Dialect menu offers Standard Arabic, Levantine, Egyptian, Gulf, Moroccan and Iraqi, each with grammar lessons on what it does differently and a Basics list of its own common words, with audio by a voice of that dialect (none yet for Moroccan).
- **Progress and XP**: finished lessons show their score, earn XP for your creature, and sync between your devices.

### The creature game

**Your creatures**
- **An egg that grows as you study**: an egg on Home hatches and grows as you study, from egg to hatchling to juvenile, until it is fully grown.
- **18 creatures**: six elements (Ember, Tide, Grove, Spark, Stone and Dusk), each with three hand-drawn pixel-art creatures (an original, a cyberpunk and a fantasy one) that walk, blink, dip their heads and sleep.
- **Baby looks**: hatchlings have a baby look of their own, smaller with a bigger head, until they grow into juveniles.
- **Fully grown**: a fully grown creature leaves you a new egg and 25 coins, and can be released in the garden.
- **Special colourings**: Twin, Nocturne and Champion colourings, earned by how you study and battle, change how a creature looks.
- **Creatures page**: choose each creature's four battle moves and colouring, and which one friends see.
- **Pet your creature**: petting your creature shows one of your saved words and its meaning in a bubble.
- **Badges**: permanent badges record what you've done, such as streaks, saved words, filled study habits and a fully grown creature.

**Earning**
- **XP for studying**: looking up words (with surprise bonuses and combos), saving words, study time, flashcards and reaching your daily goal all earn XP.
- **No XP for watching YouTube**: YouTube's rules don't allow rewards for watching, so time spent on a YouTube video (YouTube Music too) earns no XP and doesn't count toward the daily goal. Looking up and saving words while you watch still earn XP, as everywhere else, and podcasts, Netflix, reels and your own files keep their listening XP.
- **XP from courses**: finished grammar lessons, examples you tap to hear and basics words you add to your cards earn XP too.
- **Daily goal and goal streak**: reaching your daily study goal (minutes of listening or reading, not counting YouTube) pays the most and builds a goal streak, which freezes can protect.
- **Levels, coins and more**: you earn levels, coins, gems, glow and hearts, and tapping any of them in the habitat's bar explains what it's for.
- **Flashcards and battles together**: flashcards and battles pay their own rewards, with a bonus for doing both in a day.

**Shop and garden**
- **Shop**: a daily selection of wearables (113 in all: hats, glasses, scarves, necklaces, things to hold, capes, packs and shirts) that fit each creature's shape, in smaller versions for hatchlings, and decorations for the habitat.
- **Battle moves in the shop**: four battle moves are on offer each day for 40 to 130 coins, bought once and usable by any of your creatures.
- **The garden**: build buildings that produce while you're away, feed your creatures, and buy supplies such as potions, XP boosts, an incubator for a second egg and mystery eggs.

**Battles**
- **Battles from your own words**: once you've saved 20 words, battles quiz you on your own words.
- **Live arena battles**: your creature runs around a big arena made fresh for each battle (meadows, forests, shores, deserts, volcanoes, caves, ruins, night fields or snow), with a small map and an arrow pointing to your rival.
- **Move how you like**: move with a controller's stick or D-pad, W A S D or the arrow keys, by clicking or holding the mouse, or by dragging a finger.
- **Every move asks a word**: each of your four moves (shown as banners placed like a controller's X Y A B buttons) pauses the battle for a word question with a 5-second bar, where a right answer lands the move, quick ones build a combo, and a wrong or slow one fails.
- **The ground matters**: some ground slows you, is slippery, burns, heals or helps one element, and some moves change the ground.
- **Elements**: each element is strong against two others, so picking the right creature matters.
- **Which words**: "Last 100 words" asks your 100 newest words with every creature at the same level, and "All-time" asks any saved word, with creatures growing stronger the more words you know.
- **Kinds of battle**: wild encounters (with potions), rental duels with this week's shared creatures that count for your skill rating, gyms, and challenges against friends' teams.
- **Gyms**: beat ten gym leaders in order, from Pip to Zenith the champion, each harder than the last with less time for each question, opening the next and paying coins and XP the first time, with rematches any time.
- **Weekly rules**: friend battles have a rule each week, such as "Basic moves only", "No healing" or "One on one".
- **Sound effects switch**: Settings can turn off the game's chimes and jingles while keeping the game.
- **Game off switch**: a Game features switch in Settings turns the whole creature game off or on.

**Fishing**
- **Fish for the words that are due**: the Fishing tile on Home says how many fish are waiting. Each word due for review is a fish shadow in the pond, bigger for words you often miss; tap the water to cast, and when a fish bites, pick the word that means what its card says. Right lands the fish, wrong or slow lets it get away, and every answer counts as a review.
- **Rarity**: each fish is rolled on ten tiers, Common to Eternal. A Legendary or better fights back for one or two more right answers; one that breaks free waits in that pond all day.
- **Six ponds**: one per element with three fish each (18 in all). Two are open at the start; the others open with gym leaders beaten or fish caught. Your creature is luckier in the water of its own element.
- **Basket, aquarium and Fishdex**: sell fish for coins, feed one to your creature for a heart, keep up to six in an aquarium friends can see, and fill the Fishdex with every fish at every tier.
- **Pearls and rods**: rarer fish hold pearls, which buy better rods that make every cast luckier.

**Playing with friends**
- **Challenge friends**: challenge a friend's team to a battle.
- **Leagues**: climb from Bronze to Silver, Gold and Diamond.
- **Guild raids**: wear down a weekly boss together with your guild.
- **Gifts**: send gifts to friends.
- **Friends see your game**: beside each friend's name are their language, league, today's XP, flashcard streak and favourite creature, and tapping any part explains it.

### Friends, chat and guilds
- **An optional account**: connect with Google and pick a username, with a "Sign in with Google" button on the Friends tab.
- **Friends**: add friends by username or with Find a friend, accept or decline requests, and see changes live.
- **Profiles**: profile photos, a profile card that folds away, and an online dot only friends can see.
- **Chat**: text messages and voice notes of up to 2 minutes, with unread counts and notifications on every version.
- **Disappearing messages**: messages disappear once they're seen, played or added, or after 30 days unopened.
- **Share words**: send saved words or a whole folder to a friend, with a short audio clip of each sentence, and they add them in one tap.
- **Share what you're watching**: send anything from your Library to a friend, and it arrives with its words ready.
- **What I'm studying**: a card on your profile shows up to three things you're studying (a podcast episode, a YouTube video, a reel, a web page, or a Spotify, Apple Music or IMDb link), which friends open in Tamkeen's players.
- **Feed, Friends and Guilds**: the Friends tab has a Feed of friends' study cards, your friends and requests, and your guilds.
- **Guilds**: create a guild with a name and tag, invite friends, and join as many as you like.

### Sync across devices and Google Drive
- **Your library in your own Drive**: podcasts, transcripts, saved words, explanations, review progress and game progress are kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive, so all your devices share one library.
- **Within seconds**: changes reach your other devices within seconds, and every version has a refresh button.
- **Settings travel too**: your AI keys, controller buttons, Instagram collections, followed channels and starred sites reach your other devices.
- **The same audio everywhere**: each transcribed episode keeps a small copy of its audio, so other devices play exactly what was transcribed.
- **Delete everywhere at once**: deleting an episode, video or file removes it from every device.
- **One Google sign-in**: in the web app, one Google sign-in covers both Drive sync and Friends, and renews itself.
- **Backups**: export your library to a backup file, import one, or open the data folder *(desktop app)*.
- **Reset Tamkeen**: start the app again as if it were new, without deleting anything, and everything comes back when you sign in again.
- **Your keys stay yours**: your AI keys are kept on your own devices and your own Drive, left out of backups, and photos you scan go only to your own AI provider.

### Settings and personal choices
- **Settings in sections**: Settings has nine sections (Languages, Word definitions, Listening & transcripts, Card voices, Look & feel, Controller, Account & sync, Apps & connections, Data & updates), each showing its current setting.
- **Search Settings**: a search box finds any setting and opens it, and "More options" holds the expert settings.
- **Theme and text size**: choose a light, dark or system theme and the size of the app's text *(the web app is always dark)*.
- **Changelog and About**: Settings shows the changelog of every version, an About page and the open-source licences.

### Controller and accessibility
- **Game controller everywhere**: a USB or Bluetooth controller (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch Pro and most others) works on every screen except the built-in web browser, and does nothing while you type in a text box.
- **Like the Xbox dashboard**: the stick or D-pad moves a highlight, A selects, B goes back, X and Y do the screen's extra things, the bumpers switch tabs, the triggers jump a page, and Menu opens more options.
- **Controller in the players**: play and pause, skip, change line, hear a line again, loop, slow down, and move a highlight word by word to open, play and save words.
- **Controller on review cards**: turn the card, answer with the bumpers, play the word or sentence, Say it, walk through the card's boxes, and press the left stick for the Mnemonic maker.
- **Controller on Netflix's pages**: move between Netflix's titles and buttons, pick, go back and scroll, with a small guide in the corner.
- **Button guide**: a strip at the foot of the screen shows what each button does there, and a small controller button in the corner shows or hides it.
- **Choose your own buttons**: Settings → Controller shows a drawing of your controller with a line from each button to its job on the screen you pick, names buttons as printed on your controller, and lets any button be given another job.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: on a computer, keys work the players and the battles, such as R for back to the start of the sentence and ? for help.
- **Calm options**: sound effects, vibration and the whole game can each be switched off.

### Where Tamkeen runs
- **Desktop app**: for Windows, Mac and Linux, and the only version that transcribes on the device itself.
- **Android app**: installed from a file attached to each release.
- **iPhone app**: installed with AltStore rather than the App Store, and updated with every release.
- **Web app**: Tamkeen's phone screens as a website in any browser, which can be added to your home screen.
- **Every feature in every version**: all four versions do the same things, apart from what a device can't do.
- **Updates**: "Update now" downloads and installs a new version by itself *(desktop app and Android)*, the iPhone explains how to update with AltStore, and the web app shows what's new the first time a new version opens.
- **Inside other apps**: other apps such as Onyx Calendar can open a YouTube video straight in Tamkeen's player, and on the computer Tamkeen's player can appear inside them *(not in the web app)*.
