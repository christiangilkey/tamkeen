Tamkeen for Netflix

A browser extension for Chrome and Edge on a computer. On Netflix it adds Tamkeen's subtitle bar: the subtitles in the language you're learning become words you can click, with the whole transcript beside the video.

Download the extension tamkeen-netflix.zip · 0.38.0 · 2.7 MB

Add it to Chrome or Edge

It isn't in the browsers' extension stores yet, so it is added by hand, once.

  1. Unzip the file you downloaded (right-click it, then Extract All). Keep the folder somewhere it can stay, such as Documents.
  2. Open the extensions page: type chrome://extensions in Chrome's address bar, or edge://extensions in Edge's.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder (the one with manifest.json in it).
  5. Tamkeen opens in a new tab: that hands the extension the language you're learning and your settings. Then choose Netflix in Tamkeen, or click the extension's button in the toolbar.

Firefox

Open about:debugging, choose This Firefox, then Load Temporary Add-on, and pick manifest.json in the unzipped folder. Firefox removes it again when it closes, until the extension is in Firefox's add-on store.

Updating it

Download it again, unzip it over the old folder, then click the round arrow on Tamkeen for Netflix on the extensions page.

Your accounts stay yours

You sign in to Netflix on Netflix's own site: the extension never sees your password. Netflix's subtitles and the words you save stay in your own Tamkeen library, and are never shared.
