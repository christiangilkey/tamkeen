Tamkeen

Learn a language from real podcasts, videos and web pages, one word at a time.

A free, open-source app for learning Spanish, Arabic, French, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Italian, Tagalog, Brazilian Portuguese, Catalan, Bisaya, Thai, German, Persian, Hindi or English from real material. Subscribe to any podcast by its RSS feed and transcribe episodes on your own computer with OpenAI's Whisper. Then read along as each word lights up while it's spoken.

  • Word-level transcripts in the language you choose, made locally. Your audio never leaves your computer.
  • Tap any word to hear just that word and see what it means in that sentence, using your own AI provider key (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Grok).
  • Slow playback, sentence looping and auto-scrolling text (right-to-left for Arabic).
  • Vocab list that saves each word with its sentence and audio, and exports to Anki.
  • Optional sync between your computers and phone through a private folder in your own Google Drive.

Download for Windows, macOS or Linux
